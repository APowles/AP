# KTR Studio changes to roll out to every KTR

Pilot KTRs: Parcoursup (n=9), Le rap français / Booba and Oxmo Puccino (n=24), Lucie Aubrac (n=70).
Record kept from 6 Nov 2026. Add a line for every change. Roll out to all KTRs only after the full sweep of these three.

## Layout and stage names (global code: already applies to every KTR)
- Stage 'Key facts' renamed "Key ideas and facts" (shown in tabs, route text, print heading, tour step).
- Key facts order: Les idées clés first, then people, places, dates, events, laws, figures.
- Go Deeper order: Study the picture, then Optional extra: further facts to mention, then Optional extra: media and images.
- First Go Deeper step renamed "Study the picture" (was "The picture").
- Go Deeper media step renamed "Optional extra: media and images" (was "To illustrate").
- Go Deeper "At a glance" step renamed "Optional extra: further facts to mention" (was "At a glance").
- Go Deeper spot-the-details: button "👆 Click here, then observe" (was "Note the following details").
- Numbers reveal one point at a time on tap; "Show all points" button; demo text only on "Show the text" (was "Show the words").
- Go Deeper: no Key facts invitation until the last Go Deeper step.

## Learn the story (Overview)
- Order: Read & listen → Gaps → Match the titles → Recap the story.
- Match: correct title keeps its readable colour with a thick green border and a green tick by "Bien ! Right."
- Recap the story: whole pastel box reveals the French at the same size, in blue. Instructions larger.
- Gaps: no autoplay on reveal; clue shows only the bold English gap.

## Practise it: source picker (6 Nov 2026)
- Nothing is preselected when the page opens. The last choice is no longer remembered between visits (it was stored in localStorage).
- Until a source is chosen, every activity is greyed out, with the line "Choose one source above first."
- Whole KTR is labelled "Whole KTR (all of the above)" and sits first.
- View & Print for Whole KTR still omits the Compare and Quotes sections, and the Photos section, which the KTR's layout includes. To be fixed in the sheet renderer (kpSheetOne) for all KTRs.

## Speed cards: highlighted, not bold (6 Nov 2026)
- The "translate only" cue now reads "Translate only the highlighted part" and the tag reads "TRANSLATE ONLY THE HIGHLIGHTED PART" (was "bold").
- The asked part is shown with a pale gold background and bold type, not underlining, which was too faint.
- Long sentences: keep only the asked phrase highlighted; the rest of the sentence stays as context. To check on each KTR when rolled out.

## View & Print: whole KTR (6 Nov 2026)
- The Whole KTR printed sheet now includes the Compare, Quotes (Les phrases clés) and Photos sections, as well as Qui, Quoi, Quand, Combien and Les idées clés. Previously Compare, Quotes and Photos were left out.

- Tier buttons show one tier at a time. A new All button shows every tier, with a heading for each.
- Shuffle and Unticked first work only within the tier on screen (or within each tier under All), so the tiers stay together.
- Back to logical order restores the tier-first order.

## Practise it: reasons for greyed-out activities (6 Nov 2026)

- Until a source is chosen, each activity says "Choose a source first".
