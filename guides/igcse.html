Part 1. Read this first: how every Eton app works

All of the Eton apps share the same look, the same buttons and the same keys. Learn them once here and you will know them everywhere. Later parts describe each app on its own, grouped by level and then by language.

1.1 Getting into an app

  • Each app is one web page. Your teacher gives you the link (usually in OneNote or Teams). Open it in your browser on your iPad or computer.

  • Each page name starts with Eton. The exact page name and link are printed at the top of every app's entry in this guide.

  • If your teacher publishes a newer version, the page refreshes itself once. Your saved answers are kept.

  • The AP Tests page and the set tests need an internet connection. Pages that only drill are happy once they have loaded.

  • Tap 🔊 Voices (top of every app) to check which voice reads each language. On an iPad the voice sounds far better if you download it first: Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.

  • Tap ⌂ Home to go back to the start screen. It stops any audio.

  • Tap ← Back (next to Home, once you have left the first screen) to go back exactly one step, for example from a drill to its set-up screen, or from a set-up screen to the menu. It also stops any audio.

1.3 The self-marking drill

This is the heart of every app. You mark yourself, so be honest. Nobody can see your score and nobody is fooled by an inflated one.

  1. Press ▶ Start Drill. A card appears and the question is read aloud. A timer bar shrinks.

  2. Say the answer out loud (or write it). Do this before you look.

  3. Press Show Answer, or press →. The answer appears and is read aloud. Press 🔊 Replay to hear it again. If the timer runs out the answer appears by itself ("Time's up!").

  4. Mark yourself with the three buttons or the arrow keys (table below).

  5. At the end you get your score. Cards you marked as major errors come back for a second go.

Mark Key What happens
✗ Major error ← You did not know it, or it was badly wrong. The card is saved on your error list and comes back in the Major Error Drill.
~ Nearly ↓ Close, with a small slip (an accent, an ending). The card comes back 2 or 3 cards later in the same drill.
✓ Got it → Right. It counts as a point.

Showing the answer: press → (in most apps Space or Enter also work). Then ←, ↓ and → mark the card.

  • The bar at the top shows your running score (✓, ~ and ✗ counts).

  • ▶ Errors opens your error list so far.

  • ⚙ Adjust (where you see it) pauses the drill so you can change the direction or the number of cards. Press ▶ Apply & resume to carry on.

  • A Hint switch (verb apps) shows the infinitive on the card.

  • In the Major Error Drill you can also press ⏭ Ignore to skip a card with no penalty.

The end screen

  • Your score as x / total and a percentage.

  • Either "No major errors: clean sweep" or "N cards to revisit". Copy list copies those cards so you can paste them into OneNote.

  • ▶ Drill major errors again, ↻ Do same set again (all the cards of your selection, not just the first few) and ⌂ Home.

Tip Use Nearly honestly. It is the button that brings a card back quickly, and that repetition is what makes the card stick.

1.4 Leisure Mode (☕)

Leisure Mode is for soaking up the language. Make your selection, press ☕ Leisure Mode and sit back. Each card is read aloud, there is a pause for you to answer in your head or out loud, then the answer is shown and read, and the next card follows. It loops for ever, reshuffling. There is no marking, no score and no error list.

  • Pace: the − and + buttons change the thinking pause while it plays. Make it longer for long sentences.

  • ⏸ Pause / ▶ Resume: or press Space.

  • 🔊 Replay repeats the current card. ⌂ Home stops.

Tip Leisure Mode is excellent in the evening, on a walk or on a journey. Try answering out loud before the answer arrives.

1.5 ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge (the tile game)

A fast game for pairs or teams. It fills the screen with numbered tiles. Each tile shows a prompt (usually English).

  1. Choose the settings: Level (Easy / Medium / Hard / Mixed), Tiles (6, 9, 12 or 16), Time, Teams (Off, 2, 3 or 4; click a team name to rename it), Each turn (20 seconds to 1 minute) and Direction.

  2. Say the number of a tile and then say the answer aloud.

  3. Click the tile (or type its number) to reveal and hear the correct answer.

  4. Press ✓ Right (the tile greys out and you score a point) or ✗ Wrong (a new item replaces it).

  5. Beeps sound at one third and two thirds of the time. A buzzer sounds at the end.

  6. In team games, a wrong answer passes the turn to the next team at once, and a flashing sign shows whose go it is. The team with the most tiles wins.

When the game ends you can ▶ Play the N left on screen, ✗ Play the N that were wrong, 📋 Go through the wrong ones (no timer) or deal a 🔀 New full set.

Other buttons: ❓ How to play, 🔀 New set, ⏸ Pause timer, 👁 Reveal all, 🖨 Print sheet, 🔊 Sound on. The game also has its own Generate Test and Eton Spot Test buttons that use the tiles you were playing with.

A Speed session is the same game but set by your teacher through a link or code. It is locked to your teacher's choice, starts with 2 teams, 16 tiles, English to the foreign language, 20 seconds a turn and a 3 minute game, and you stay in it until your teacher says stop. There is no leaving it (see Part 2).

1.6 Tests you can make yourself

Press 📝 Generate Test on any set-up screen. A panel opens with Level (Easy / Medium / Hard / Mixed), Questions (10, 20, 25 or Custom) and Eton Spot Test · items per box (1, 2 or 3). In the verb apps there is also a Sentences tab. There are three different test buttons:

Button What it gives you
📝 Generate Test A printable test sheet and an answer sheet. Use Copy test sheet, Copy answer sheet or 🖨 Print / PDF. Good for homework or testing a friend.
📋 Eton Spot Test A sheet of 5 boxes, for your teacher to dictate in class. It is always marked out of 10. You will normally meet it in the classroom, not at home.
⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted The same test done on screen, then marked by you and saved as a PDF. This is the one to use for serious practice. See 1.7.

1.7 The timed “Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted”, step by step

  1. Press ⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted. A box asks you to Choose your level. Leave it on Mixed unless your teacher has told you otherwise. Your level is printed at the top of your PDF.

  2. Choose ⏱ Timed (use this in Birley Schools, or when your teacher has set the test as an EW) or 🏠 Untimed. You cannot change your mind once you start. The clock starts the moment you tap. It can be paused only for a real emergency (a fire alarm, for example): press ⏸ Pause at the top right, type (or write with your Apple Pencil) why you are pausing, then press ▶ Resume. While paused the clock is stopped and the test is hidden, you must still stay on the page (leaving it still sounds the siren and is logged), and every pause is printed on your PDF. While a timed test is open your iPad stays awake (it will not dim or lock itself); if you lock it yourself with the side button, that is logged like leaving the page. If the clock at the top of the screen distracts you, tap it to make it smaller (tap again to make it bigger); it stays on screen. Blank answers score 0. No timed test runs longer than 4½ minutes: a test of short answers (words or verb forms) can have up to 20 items, while longer answers give fewer. Where your teacher's list gives more than one accepted answer for an English prompt (for example "living room": la salle de séjour / le salon), the mark scheme now shows them all. Spellings that are right either way are listed too (for example l'aller simple, with or without the hyphen).

  3. Answer each item. Each has two tabs: ✍️ Handwrite (Apple Pencil, black ink) and ⌨️ Type. Only type if you type in exams or have no Pencil.

  4. When finished, press 🔒 I've attempted all N: lock in. In a timed test you get 30 seconds of grace after the time ends, and then your answers lock themselves.

  5. Press ✔ Show answers & mark. Correct your own answer in red first (tick it if perfect, or underline the errors). You cannot choose a mark until there is red on the answer. Once you have used red pen, the app reminds you in red to tap a mark. If those red reminders are too much, press 🔕 Turn off the red “tap your mark” alerts (it appears the first time, or in the marking box); press 🔔 Turn the red alerts back on to bring them back. Your choice is remembered on that iPad. Without the alerts, an unmarked answer still scores 0.

  6. Now give yourself a mark. Single words and forms: 2 Right, 1 Nearly (a missing accent, a spelling slip, a wrong ending or gender), 0 Wrong. Sentences and structures: 4 Perfect, 3 Mostly right, 2 About half right, 1 Only a word or two right, 0 Nothing right. The test is out of 10. In a timed test the marking time is limited too, and anything you have not marked scores 0.

  7. Write in the 📝 What I learned box. This is the most important part: what caught you out, and how will you remember it? In Birley Schools you get a couple of minutes; in your House take as long as you need. Try to find 10 things to write (5 is the minimum), numbered 1 to 10: even a single piece of vocabulary or a rule counts. The PDF button unlocks only after you have written for 1 minute.

  8. Press 📄 Save my work as PDF and file it (see 1.9).

Careful Do not leave the page during a test. If you switch to another app or tab, use Split View, reload or press Back, a loud 3 second siren sounds and a red warning flashes. The attempt is logged and recorded, and the PDF says how many times you left and for how long.

If you are not sure about a mark, do not stop to ask your teacher. Mark it wrong if in doubt and ask afterwards.

  • Your attempt number is shown ("ATTEMPT n ON THIS TEST").

  • If a page reloads by accident, reopening the same test within 12 hours picks up where you were. There is a Start afresh option.

  • When you have finished, marked and saved your PDF, you can press 🔄 Take a fresh test at the bottom of the page for new questions with the same settings. The test you have just done is cleared, so save its PDF first. Teacher-set tests do not have this button.

1.8 Writing with the Apple Pencil (Eton PEN)

  • Written answers have two tabs: ✍️ Handwrite (the default) and ⌨️ Type.

  • Pen tools: Pen, Thin / Medium, Eraser, Undo, Clear and + space (adds more room).

  • When the app detects your Pencil, your finger no longer draws: it scrolls the page.

  • Write in black ink. Once marking begins the black ink locks, and a 🖊 Red pen: corrections appears. All corrections go in red. The eraser then removes red only.

  • Typed answers get a red "Corrections" box instead.

1.9 Saving your work to OneNote

Marked papers and timed tests end with 📄 Save my work as PDF (or 📄 Save marked paper as PDF).

  1. The app asks for your name once and remembers it. It also gives you a OneNote page title (date, then a clear title such as "Eton Spot Test on VV10a") with a Copy button.

  2. On an iPad the print screen opens. Do not print. Tap the Share button, then OneNote.

  3. Choose your Div's Class OneNote, then your name, then Class Work (work done in Birley Schools) or EW (work done in your House).

  4. Paste the title you copied, then press Send.

  5. In OneNote, tap the PDF icon and choose Printout so that the pages show in full. (The paper-clip File button only adds a small attachment box.)

On a computer: Save as PDF, then in OneNote choose Insert > File Printout. If the share route fails on an iPad, use Save to Files, then in OneNote Insert > PDF.

1.10 Grammar recaps and "Now I can" checklists

Every grammar chapter in the Gr EWs apps has an Eton grammar recap: a clear explanation of the rules, with examples. Read the recap before you drill the chapter. All recaps use the same colours, and a colour key sits at the top of each one:

Colour What it means
Core rule (blue) The rule itself.
Examples & how to say (green) Examples, with how to say them.
Tips & patterns (amber) Tips and patterns that help you remember.
Exceptions: learn by heart (red) The awkward ones. Learn them.
Watch out (yellow) The points to be careful about.
Lists & vocab (white) Lists and vocabulary to learn.

Each recap also has two special boxes:

  • Soon I will be able to… (grey dashed box, near the top): what you are about to learn. It is for reading, and cannot be ticked.

  • Now I can… (bright yellow box at the very end): a checklist for after you have drilled the chapter. Tick each box when you can do it, then press the red Check button to compare with a model answer (the button then says Hide). The prompts are in English. The French, Italian or Spanish appears only in the model answer.

Your ticks are saved on that device and in that browser only. They do not go to your teacher, they do not show on your other devices, and clearing the site data wipes them. They do not print either. You find the recaps in the IGCSE app (section 3.4, Vocab and Gr EWs).

Tip Be strict. Tick a box only if you could do it from memory, then press Check to see whether you really could.

1.11 Things to ignore

Every app contains tools that belong to your teacher. You will never need them, and you should leave them alone:

  • The round photo in the header (it switches on teacher mode).

  • Any button marked ✎ Edit, ✏️ Edit Vocab, Print Content, Study Sheet, ⬇ Export HTML or 👩‍🏫 Teacher.

  • Any button starting 🔑 (making test links). You will not even see these unless your teacher has set up a key on that device.

  • The small Teacher button at the foot of the AP Tests page.

Part 2. Tests set by your teacher (AP Tests)

▶ Open AP Tests (opens the app in your browser)

When your teacher sets you a test (in class, or as an EW) you receive either a link or a code. Both open the same thing. Every set test is timed and submitted.

2.1 How to open a set test

  1. With a link (from OneNote or Teams): tap it. The right app opens and your test is ready.

  2. With a code: open AP Tests. You will see Enter your test code. Type the code (for example STF101) and tap Go. The box turns your letters into capitals.

  3. To try the routine without risk, type DEMO101. It is 10 everyday words in French, Spanish and Italian in 1 minute 30. Nobody is expected to know them all.

  • If you see "There is no test called X", check the code with your teacher. Codes your teacher has only just added can take about a minute to work.

  • If you see "The list of test codes could not be loaded", check your internet connection.

  • If a page reloaded part way through, a ▶ Continue your test button appears and your own answers are back.

2.2 What the codes mean

Code starts Kind of test
ST… (e.g. STF101, STS101) A timed Spot Test or past paper. The third letter is the language: F French, S Spanish, I Italian.
SP… (e.g. SPF101, SPS101) A Speed session: the tile game, locked to your teacher's choice.
CLF… and CLI… A Listening class sheet (CLF for French, CLI for Italian; see Part 3).
RTF… A timed Reading paper (see Part 3).
ORF…, ORS…, ORI… A recorded oral on one card that your teacher has fixed for you (F French, S Spanish, I Italian; see 2.3 and Part 3).
DRS… (e.g. DRS101) A set drill. The app opens straight into a drill that your teacher has chosen. No timer, nothing logged. Press ✕ Free practice to leave it. So far this is in Eton F Spanish Vocab.

Eton often sets the same set as two or three practice tests (named, for example, "VV11b PRACTICE 1, 2 and 3") for you to do at home, then the ACTUAL TEST in Birley Schools. Your questions may differ from your neighbour's. Between them, the practice tests cover the whole selection your teacher chose, and the ACTUAL TEST is a fresh random draw from all of it, so learning just one practice test will not be enough.

2.3 Doing a set spot test

It works like the timed test in section 1.7, with these differences:

  • There is no untimed option. Press ⏱ Start: N minutes. The clock starts when you tap OK. It can be paused only for a real emergency: press ⏸ Pause, write why, then ▶ Resume. Every pause is printed on your PDF.

  • The screen says ✅ TEST SET BY YOUR TEACHER with the code.

  • Your PDF carries the code, your times, your attempt number and your level. Use the OneNote page title exactly as the app gives it.

Speed sessions

  • The game opens locked: no list chooser, no way back to the app, no tests and no printing.

  • There is no session timer. You stay until your teacher says stop. ⏹ End session (ask your teacher first) exists, but ask first.

  • Leaving the page sounds the siren and is logged.

Set past papers

A code for an A Level Paper 1 question (for example Q5 Phrases à compléter) sends you to the Eton A Level French Oral Topics & Paper 1 Practice page, timed.

Recorded orals (OR codes)

  • A code starting OR (for example ORI101, an Italian oral) opens the oral app with one card fixed by your teacher. You cannot change it.

  • The 🎙 Record my oral box opens by itself, headed "Set by your teacher (ORI101): this is your card. Record your answers, then follow the steps to send your PDF and recording."

  • Then follow the recording steps in section 3.4 (the Structures app). Put both the PDF and the recording on your EW page in OneNote.

3.1 French

Eton French Verbs

▶ Open Eton French Verbs (opens the app in your browser)

What it is for: drilling, gap-fill sentences and tests on French verbs. The subtitle reads "Conjugation drills · Sentences · Tests · Audio". The home screen says "Ready to conjugate?"

Choose a verb set

  • 📚 Quizlet: about 100 verbs, all six tenses plus compound tenses. This is the default.

  • 🎓 Specialist: 35 less common verbs (acquérir, naître, croître, mentir, peindre, valoir, croire, tenir, mettre, mourir, s'asseoir…).

  • ⚡ Both: the two together.

  • 🧩 Top Structures: 586 Eton Advanced Verb Structures, set phrases such as "profiter de X", conjugated in every tense. Tick Display structure hint to see the French structure as well as the English.

  • 🏅 F Michaelmas Trials: all the Quizlet verbs, for the Michaelmas trials. Only three tenses are offered: Infinitive, Present and Perfect, and the Perfect has only the verbs that take avoir.

  • 🏅 E Block Michaelmas Trials: all the Quizlet verbs, with Infinitive, Present, Perfect (every verb, including être and reflexive verbs), Imperfect and Future.

  • 🏅 F Block Summer Trials and 🏅 E Block Summer Trials: the same verbs and tenses as E Block Michaelmas Trials.

  • 📋 Trials Part I paper: with any of the four Trials sets selected, the Generate Test panel has a "Trials Part I paper" button. It makes a printable 40-question paper (sections A to D) with a separate answer page. Each section draws on a fixed Importance group: A uses Useful verbs, B uses Essential, C uses Important, and D uses Essential again, avoiding any verb whose infinitive already appears elsewhere on the paper. The Importance and Level choosers are ignored for this paper.

Use Specialist and Top Structures when Eton tells you to. The Importance buttons (Essential, Important, Useful) work inside these four sets too, so you can sort the trials verbs by importance, and 📖 View & Print can group them by tier.

How important is each verb? Under Verb set there is an Importance row: Essential, Important, Essential + Important, Useful and All verbs. It works on the 📚 Quizlet verbs. Essential (40) is the verbs to learn and be able to use yourself, such as être, avoir, aller, faire, pouvoir and vouloir. Important (30) is the next most useful. Useful (30) is worth knowing but the least likely to be needed. It is about how likely you are to need the verb when you speak or write, not how hard it is (that is Level: Easy, Medium, Hard). The same choice is used in the drill, Generate Test (Conjugation and Sentences tabs and the compound tenses test), the Speed Challenge and 📖 View & Print, where each verb has an E, I or U badge and Group by: Tier lists them in three groups. Specialist and Top Structures verbs are not graded and always stay in. A red warning shows whenever it is not on All verbs, so it cannot be missed. In ⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted a Which verbs? box comes before the Level box: leave it on All verbs unless your teacher has told you otherwise. What you choose is printed at the top of the test, next to the Level. Level (Easy, Medium, Hard) still works as well, and the two combine. In the Speed Challenge the Importance buttons are in the bar and in 📚 Choose verbs. The choice is forgotten when you close the page.

Choose a mode

  • 📑 Full (6 forms): you see the English verb, say all six persons (je, tu, il (elle), nous, vous, ils (elles)), then press Show All Forms.

  • 🎯 Single form: one English form, such as "we will answer". Press Show Answer. A Person row (All persons, je, tu…) appears only in this mode.

  • 🔤 Infinitives: "What is the French infinitive?" then Show Infinitive.

  • 💬 Sentences: a gap-fill. You see the sentence with "_______" and a coloured cue such as "je · acheter". Press the speaker to hear the sentence, then ▶ Show answer to see the whole sentence and the infinitive.

Choose tenses and other options

  • Tenses (tap to select; Present alone is the default): Present, Future, Perfect (PC), Imperfect, Conditional, Subjunctive, Infinitive, Pluperfect, Future Perfect, Conditional Perfect. All Tenses ticks everything. The subjunctive answer shows "que" or "qu'".

  • Regularity: All, Regular, Irregular.

  • Cards per session, Timer per card, and 💡 Hint: On/Off.

  • The first time, the defaults are Single form, Present, Quizlet, 10 cards and 8 seconds.

Your first session

  1. Leave the verb set on Quizlet. Choose 🎯 Single form, one or two tenses and 10 cards.

  2. Press ▶ Start Drill. Say the answer aloud, press Show, then mark with ← ↓ →.

  3. At the end use ▶ Drill major errors again. Later, try Full and say all six persons.

Generate Test in the verb apps

  • Conjugation tab: pick the verb set, tenses, regularity, person and Level. Easy is regular verbs, Medium is common irregulars, Hard is other irregulars and reflexives. Show in question (Tense, Person, Infinitive) controls what is printed.

  • Sentences tab: verb set, level, number of questions, the structure hint, and 📝 Generate Sentence Test. It prints a Student sheet and a Teacher sheet.

  • Then the three test buttons from section 1.6. The timed test is described in 1.7.

Remember 📖 View & Print gives you a list of infinitives with a search box, Copy list and Print / PDF.

Eton IGCSE French Listening (and Reading)

▶ Open Eton IGCSE French Listening (opens the app in your browser)

What it is for: Cambridge IGCSE (9–1) French Paper 1 Listening (7156) practice, with a Reading add-on for Paper 2. The page title is "Eton IGCSE Listening and Reading Papers" and the subtitle reads "35 papers · Audio".

The home screen

  • The switch at the top: 🎧 Listening and 📖 Reading. Under it is the 🇫🇷 French | 🇮🇹 Italian switch: tap Italian to open the Italian Listening app (section 3.2).

  • Under "Past Paper Bank / Listening Comprehension" is a grid of paper cards. Each shows the FR code (FR001 to FR035), the Cambridge code, the title, "37 questions · 40 marks", ▶ Start and 🖨 Answer sheet.

  • Or type a code in the box under OR ENTER A PAPER CODE and press Enter or ▶ Go.

  • 🖨 Blank answer sheet (any paper) prints an answer sheet.

  • FR001 to FR015 are real Cambridge papers (Specimen 2021 to 2025). FR016 to FR035 are AI Generated 1 to 20. Three papers (FR004 to FR006) are greyed out and cannot be opened.

Careful Questions 1 to 8 use pictures in the real exam. Here they are replaced by English word options.

The real exam plays each passage twice. Here you get one play and one replay.

Doing a Listening paper

  1. Choose a paper card (or type its code). A bar shows where you are ("Section 3 / 7"). The Jump to row moves you between sections.

  2. Press ▶ Start Listening. Nothing plays by itself. The voice is the device's French text-to-speech, not a recording, so it sounds better with a downloaded voice.

  3. After the first play the button says 🔊 Replay (1 left). After the second it says 🔊 Played ✔ and you are done with that section.

  4. Questions 1 to 8 are different: each has its own 🔊 Play button, which becomes Played ✔, and a 🔊 Play again unlocks. That is two plays per question.

  5. Answer as you listen or straight after. Multiple choice: tap a letter. Matching (Q15 to 19): use the drop-downs A to F, each letter once. "Tick two" questions (Q35 to 37): tick exactly two boxes (a third tick gives "Two boxes at most").

  6. Press ✓ Mark this section. If you have not finished you will be told ("Please answer all N questions before submitting"). Correct answers go green and wrong ones red, and replay is switched off for that section.

  7. Or press Next section: marks later → to carry on without marking. Finish & see results is always there.

Section Question type Marks

Q15–19 Matching with drop-downs 5

The Top Structure Cycle

The coloured panel beside a card is a checklist of structures to build into your answers. It has four groups: Easy Present Tense Starter (green, 8 prompts), Past Tense Anecdote (blue, 18), Detailed Opinion (purple, 22) and Future Speculation (red, 14). Tap a prompt and a sheet opens with five example phrases, each with 🔊. Use it to make sure every answer has a present, a past, an opinion and a future.

Translate 10 sentences

  1. Press ✍️ Translate 10 sentences (only on cards that have a demo). Translate each English sentence with the Pencil in black ink, or type.

  2. A gold chip on each sentence names the structure to use. Sentences 1 to 6 are from the demo. Sentences 7 to 10 are "Make it your own": one thing has been changed.

  3. Choose a mode: ⚡ Quick 5: 6 minutes (5 sentences, 2 minutes marking, out of 20), ⏱ Timed: 10 minutes, or 🏠 Untimed. In Quick 5 and Timed you can use ⏸ Pause (top right) only for a real emergency: write why, then press ▶ Resume. The pause is printed on your PDF.

  4. Press 🔒 I've attempted all N: lock in, then ✔ Show answers & mark. Correct in red, then mark each sentence 4 Perfect, 3 Mostly right, 2 About half right, 1 Only a word or two right, 0 Nothing right. It is out of 40 (20 in Quick 5).

  5. Write your What I learned notes (in Quick 5, One thing I'll remember), then save the PDF. The siren and logging apply here too.

Record my oral (EW)

Careful Your answers must be completely your own and completely different from the Demo Mode answers. Do not listen to the demo before you record. The demo is offered only after you have saved your recording.

  1. Press 🎙 Record my oral (EW). Read the checklist: quiet room, iPad an arm's length away, volume up, use the iPad speaker (no headphones), do not lock the screen or leave the page. Type your name.

  2. Press 🔈 Test my microphone and say a few words while you watch the level meter. If the bar does not move after a few seconds, the app tries other settings by itself. If it still shows no bar, press 🎧 Record 4 seconds and play back, say a few words and press play. If you hear your voice, the microphone works. If you hear nothing, press 🔧 I hear nothing: try other settings. If you see "Nothing was recorded", check Settings > your browser > Microphone, reload the page and try again.

  3. Press ● Start recording. The five questions are asked aloud and timed. On an iPad the recording pauses while the examiner is speaking, so it keeps only your answers. The questions are printed on your PDF (the set-up box may still say they will be on the recording; on an iPad they will not). The REC clock stands still while the examiner talks, so the finished length is shorter than the real time. You need Safari on the iPad.

  4. If you did not catch a question, press 🔁 Hear the question again in the red bar (or 🔁 Repeat question in the panel). The questions, the View all questions buttons and the Top Structure Cycle are hidden during a recording, as in the real exam.

  5. If you leave the page while recording and come back, a flashing red warning tells you to stay on the page. There is no siren and nothing is logged.

  6. ■ Stop and throw away ends the recording and deletes it. The yellow ■ Stop and check recording ends it straight away for a quick check only ("You stopped early, so this is only a quick check"). Use it to test your microphone, never for your real recording.

  7. At the end a player appears: listen back (on an iPad you hear your answers only). 🔁 Not happy: try again, or 📤 Share recording or 💾 Save to Files. The file is named with the date, the card and your name.

  8. Press 📄 Make my PDF (card, questions and the time each started) and follow the OneNote guide on screen: a new page in the EW folder, the PDF as a Printout and the recording added with the paper-clip File button.

Part 5. Getting the most from the apps

A simple weekly routine

  1. Learn the material for your set (vocab list, VV set, Gr EW chapter or Approved Material topic) with Read & listen, View & Print or Leisure Mode.

  2. Drill it out loud: say the answer before you press Show. Mark honestly.

  3. Re-drill your errors with ▶ Drill major errors again.

  4. Test yourself with ⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted. Write your What I learned notes properly.

  5. File the PDF in OneNote, in the right folder, with the title copied from the app.
