Start here: your two Eton apps

Two apps help you learn Spanish at home. Use them for about ten minutes at a time, little and often. You mark yourself, so be honest.

App Use it for Open it
Eton F Spanish Vocab Words and useful sentences from your course, unit by unit ▶ Open Eton F Spanish Vocab
Eton F Block Verbs and Grammar Verbs: the right ending for each person. Grammar: clear notes for beginners, with drills and tests ▶ Open Eton F Block Verbs and Grammar

The set-up screen: two steps (Vocab and Verbs)

Both apps open on two steps. 1 · What do you want to practise? Choose your set. Nothing is chosen for you, and the six picture tiles stay greyed out until you do. 2 · How do you want to practise it? Press a tile: Drill, Speed Challenge, Spell (hear it and write it; accents count), Match (pair English with Spanish against the clock), Leisure Mode or Test (the answers stay hidden until you press Show answers). Cards per session, the timer and the direction are tucked into the gold ⚙ Settings bar: tap Change to open it.

The drill, in five steps (Vocab and Verbs)

  1. Open the app from the link your teacher gave you. Choose what to practise (see below).

  2. Press the Drill tile. A card appears and is read aloud.

  3. Say the answer out loud first. Then press Show Answer (or press the → key).

  4. Mark yourself: ✗ Major error (←), ~ Nearly (↓) or ✓ Got it (→).

  5. At the end press ▶ Drill major errors again. Those are the cards that matter most.

Vocab: what to choose

  • In step 1, pick a unit and a section (such as "1A Me describo"), or press 📚 Choose several topics to tick more, for example the ★ Michaelmas Trials group.

  • In ⚙ Settings, leave Direction on English to Spanish. Later press ⇄ Swap to go Spanish to English. When you are ready for a stretch, try Mix both ways (50–50 or 70–30) to get some cards each way.

  • Start with Easy words (they look like English) and add Medium and Hard later.

Verbs: what to choose

  • In Eton F Block Verbs and Grammar, tap VERBS, then 100 Core Verbs. In step 1 choose your verb groups (start with Essential 1, the verbs to learn first), a Kind of question (try Single form) and the Present tense. The tiles wake up once you have chosen verbs and a tense. Then press Drill.

  • Add more tenses only when your teacher says so.

  • Cards, timer and Hint (shows the infinitive) are in ⚙ Settings.

  • Say the whole form aloud, for example "yo aprendo". Use Nearly for a missing accent or a wrong ending.

More things to try

  • ★ My personalised set: any card you mark ✗ Major error in a drill or in Spell joins it automatically. Choose it in step 1 and practise only your tricky cards. Press 🔗 Save my personalised set to get a link, and paste it into your own OneNote under a clear title. The set lives only on this iPad, so the link is the only safe copy.

  • ✍ Spell and 🧩 Match: Spell says it aloud and you write it (accents count). Match pairs English with Spanish against the clock.

  • ☕ Leisure Mode: cards play by themselves, question then answer, with no marking.

  • ⚡ Speed Challenge ("Vocab fast!"): a tile in step 2, and a banner at the top of the Vocab app. A tile game for a pair or team: say the Spanish, click the tile, press ✓ or ✗.

  • ✍ Submitted Spot Test: a short test you mark yourself and save as a PDF to OneNote (Class Work in Birley Schools, EW in your House). Do not leave the page in a test: an error buzz sounds. No timed test runs longer than 4½ minutes: a test of short answers (words or verb forms) can have up to 20 items, while longer answers give fewer.

If your teacher sets you a drill

  • Your teacher can send you a link. It opens Eton F Spanish Vocab straight into a drill that your teacher has chosen: the topics, the direction and the number of cards are already set.

  • A dark bar says "Drill set by your teacher". No timer, nothing logged: do it as normal. Press ✕ Free practice to choose your own topics. If the link "does not work", ask your teacher for it again.

Tip Accents matter: a missing accent costs a mark. Buttons marked ✎ Edit or 🔑 are for your teacher. The full guide explains every button.
