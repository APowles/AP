Start here: your two Eton apps

Two apps help you learn Spanish at home. Use them for about ten minutes at a time, little and often. You mark yourself, so be honest.

App Use it for Open it
Eton F Spanish Vocab Words and useful sentences from your course, unit by unit ▶ Open Eton F Spanish Vocab
Eton F Block Verbs and Grammar Verbs: the right ending for each person. Grammar: clear notes for beginners, with drills and tests ▶ Open Eton F Block Verbs and Grammar

The drill, in five steps (Vocab and Verbs)

  1. Open the app from the link your teacher gave you. Choose what to practise (see below).

  2. Press ▶ Start Drill. A card appears and is read aloud.

  3. Say the answer out loud first. Then press Show Answer (or press the → key).

  4. Mark yourself: ✗ Major error (←), ~ Nearly (↓) or ✓ Got it (→).

  5. At the end press ▶ Drill major errors again. Those are the cards that matter most.

Vocab: what to choose

  • Leave Direction on English to Spanish. Later press ⇄ Swap to go Spanish to English.

  • The ★ Michaelmas Trials topics are already ticked. Press 📚 Choose topics to practise just one unit, such as "1A Me describo".

  • Start with Easy words (they look like English) and add Medium and Hard later.

Verbs: what to choose

  • In Eton F Block Verbs and Grammar, tap VERBS. The F Block Verbs (the verbs from your course), 🎯 Single form and the Present tense are already chosen for you. Just press ▶ Start Drill.

  • Add more tenses only when your teacher says so.

  • Say the whole form aloud, for example "yo aprendo". Use Nearly for a missing accent or a wrong ending.

Two more things to try

  • ☕ Leisure Mode: cards play by themselves, question then answer, with no marking.

  • ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge ("Vocab fast!"): the banner at the top of the Vocab app. A tile game for a pair or team: say the Spanish, click the tile, press ✓ or ✗.

  • ⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted: a short test you mark yourself and save as a PDF to OneNote (Class Work in Birley Schools, EW in your House). Do not leave the page in a test: a loud siren sounds. No timed test runs longer than 4½ minutes: a test of short answers (words or verb forms) can have up to 20 items, while longer answers give fewer.

If your teacher sets you a drill

  • Your teacher can send you a link, or give you a code to type on the AP Tests page (it starts DRS, for example DRS101). It opens Eton F Spanish Vocab straight into a drill that your teacher has chosen: the topics, the direction and the number of cards are already set.

  • A dark bar says "Drill set by your teacher". No timer, nothing logged: do it as normal. Press ✕ Free practice to choose your own topics. If the link "does not work", ask your teacher for it again.

Tip Accents matter: a missing accent costs a mark. Buttons marked ✎ Edit or 🔑 are for your teacher. The full guide explains every button.
