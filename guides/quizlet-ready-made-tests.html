Quizlet verbs: ready-made tests

F Block · Eton College French Department · October 2026

There is one test for each Quizlet EW slot in the scheme of work, named like the Gr EWs (F Mich Quizlet 2 is the Quizlet EW in Michaelmas 2). Each test is about 20 questions and takes about 4 to 4½ minutes. Tap a link to open it; the clock starts only when the boy taps Start. Practice 1, Practice 2 and the Actual test are three different draws from the same verbs. Useful verbs are always French → English; every other verb is English → French. Whether marks are taken in is up to the Master.
