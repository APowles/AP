Part 1. Read this first: how every Eton app works

All of the Eton apps share the same look, the same buttons and the same keys. Learn them once here and you will know them everywhere. Later parts describe each app on its own, grouped by level and then by language.

1.1 Getting into an app

  • Each app is one web page. Your teacher gives you the link (usually in OneNote or Teams). Open it in your browser on your iPad or computer.

  • Each page name starts with Eton. The exact page name and link are printed at the top of every app's entry in this guide.

  • If your teacher publishes a newer version, the page refreshes itself once. Your saved answers are kept.

  • The AP Tests page and the set tests need an internet connection. Pages that only drill are happy once they have loaded.

  • Tap 🔊 Voices (top of every app) to check which voice reads each language. On an iPad the voice sounds far better if you download it first: Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.

  • Tap ⌂ Home to go back to the start screen. It stops any audio.

  • Tap ← Back (next to Home, once you have left the first screen) to go back exactly one step, for example from a drill to its set-up screen, or from a set-up screen to the menu. It also stops any audio.

1.2 The set-up screen (the "Ready to practise?" page)

Almost every app opens on a set-up screen. You choose what to practise, then press the big ▶ Start Drill button. The controls have the same names everywhere:

1.3 The self-marking drill

This is the heart of every app. You mark yourself, so be honest. Nobody can see your score and nobody is fooled by an inflated one.

  1. Press ▶ Start Drill. A card appears and the question is read aloud. A timer bar shrinks.

  2. Say the answer out loud (or write it). Do this before you look.

  3. Press Show Answer, or press →. The answer appears and is read aloud. Press 🔊 Replay to hear it again. If the timer runs out the answer appears by itself ("Time's up!").

  4. Mark yourself with the three buttons or the arrow keys (table below).

  5. At the end you get your score. Cards you marked as major errors come back for a second go.

Mark Key What happens
✗ Major error ← You did not know it, or it was badly wrong. The card is saved on your error list and comes back in the Major Error Drill.
~ Nearly ↓ Close, with a small slip (an accent, an ending). The card comes back 2 or 3 cards later in the same drill.
✓ Got it → Right. It counts as a point.

Showing the answer: press → (in most apps Space or Enter also work). Then ←, ↓ and → mark the card.

  • The bar at the top shows your running score (✓, ~ and ✗ counts).

  • ▶ Errors opens your error list so far.

  • ⚙ Adjust (where you see it) pauses the drill so you can change the direction or the number of cards. Press ▶ Apply & resume to carry on.

  • A Hint switch (verb apps) shows the infinitive on the card.

  • In the Major Error Drill you can also press ⏭ Ignore to skip a card with no penalty.

The end screen

  • Your score as x / total and a percentage.

  • Either "No major errors: clean sweep" or "N cards to revisit". Copy list copies those cards so you can paste them into OneNote.

  • ▶ Drill major errors again, ↻ Do same set again (all the cards of your selection, not just the first few) and ⌂ Home.

Tip Use Nearly honestly. It is the button that brings a card back quickly, and that repetition is what makes the card stick.

1.4 Leisure Mode (☕)

Leisure Mode is for soaking up the language. Make your selection, press ☕ Leisure Mode and sit back. Each card is read aloud, there is a pause for you to answer in your head or out loud, then the answer is shown and read, and the next card follows. It loops for ever, reshuffling. There is no marking, no score and no error list.

  • Pace: the − and + buttons change the thinking pause while it plays. Make it longer for long sentences.

  • ⏸ Pause / ▶ Resume: or press Space.

  • 🔊 Replay repeats the current card. ⌂ Home stops.

Tip Leisure Mode is excellent in the evening, on a walk or on a journey. Try answering out loud before the answer arrives.

1.5 ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge (the tile game)

A fast game for pairs or teams. It fills the screen with numbered tiles. Each tile shows a prompt (usually English).

  1. Choose the settings: Level (Easy / Medium / Hard / Mixed), Tiles (6, 9, 12 or 16), Time, Teams (Off, 2, 3 or 4; click a team name to rename it), Each turn (20 seconds to 1 minute) and Direction.

  2. Say the number of a tile and then say the answer aloud.

  3. Click the tile (or type its number) to reveal and hear the correct answer.

  4. Press ✓ Right (the tile greys out and you score a point) or ✗ Wrong (a new item replaces it).

  5. Beeps sound at one third and two thirds of the time. A buzzer sounds at the end.

  6. In team games, a wrong answer passes the turn to the next team at once, and a flashing sign shows whose go it is. The team with the most tiles wins.

When the game ends you can ▶ Play the N left on screen, ✗ Play the N that were wrong, 📋 Go through the wrong ones (no timer) or deal a 🔀 New full set.

Other buttons: ❓ How to play, 🔀 New set, ⏸ Pause timer, 👁 Reveal all, 🖨 Print sheet, 🔊 Sound on. The game also has its own Generate Test and Eton Spot Test buttons that use the tiles you were playing with.

A Speed session is the same game but set by your teacher through a link or code. It is locked to your teacher's choice, starts with 2 teams, 16 tiles, English to the foreign language, 20 seconds a turn and a 3 minute game, and you stay in it until your teacher says stop. There is no leaving it (see Part 2).

1.6 Tests you can make yourself

Press 📝 Generate Test on any set-up screen. A panel opens with Level (Easy / Medium / Hard / Mixed), Questions (10, 20, 25 or Custom) and Eton Spot Test · items per box (1, 2 or 3). In the verb apps there is also a Sentences tab. There are three different test buttons:

Button What it gives you
📝 Generate Test A printable test sheet and an answer sheet. Use Copy test sheet, Copy answer sheet or 🖨 Print / PDF. Good for homework or testing a friend.
📋 Eton Spot Test A sheet of 5 boxes, for your teacher to dictate in class. It is always marked out of 10. You will normally meet it in the classroom, not at home.
⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted The same test done on screen, then marked by you and saved as a PDF. This is the one to use for serious practice. See 1.7.

1.7 The timed “Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted”, step by step

  1. Press ⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted. A box asks you to Choose your level. Leave it on Mixed unless your teacher has told you otherwise. Your level is printed at the top of your PDF.

  2. Choose ⏱ Timed (use this in Birley Schools, or when your teacher has set the test as an EW) or 🏠 Untimed. You cannot change your mind once you start. The clock starts the moment you tap and cannot be paused. If the clock at the top of the screen distracts you, tap it to make it smaller (tap again to make it bigger); it stays on screen. Blank answers score 0. No timed test runs longer than 4½ minutes: a test of short answers (words or verb forms) can have up to 20 items, while longer answers give fewer.

  3. Answer each item. Each has two tabs: ✍️ Handwrite (Apple Pencil, black ink) and ⌨️ Type. Only type if you type in exams or have no Pencil.

  4. When finished, press 🔒 I've attempted all N: lock in. In a timed test you get 30 seconds of grace after the time ends, and then your answers lock themselves.

  5. Press ✔ Show answers & mark. Correct your own answer in red first (tick it if perfect, or underline the errors). You cannot choose a mark until there is red on the answer. Once you have used red pen, the app reminds you in red to tap a mark. If those red reminders are too much, press 🔕 Turn off the red “tap your mark” alerts (it appears the first time, or in the marking box); press 🔔 Turn the red alerts back on to bring them back. Your choice is remembered on that iPad. Without the alerts, an unmarked answer still scores 0.

  6. Now give yourself a mark. Single words and forms: 2 Right, 1 Nearly (a missing accent, a spelling slip, a wrong ending or gender), 0 Wrong. Sentences and structures: 4 Perfect, 3 Mostly right, 2 About half right, 1 Only a word or two right, 0 Nothing right. The test is out of 10. In a timed test the marking time is limited too, and anything you have not marked scores 0.

  7. Write in the 📝 What I learned box. This is the most important part: what caught you out, and how will you remember it? In Birley Schools you get a couple of minutes; in your House take as long as you need. Try to find 10 things to write (5 is the minimum), numbered 1 to 10: even a single piece of vocabulary or a rule counts. The PDF button unlocks only after you have written for 1 minute.

  8. Press 📄 Save my work as PDF and file it (see 1.9).

Careful Do not leave the page during a test. If you switch to another app or tab, use Split View, reload or press Back, a loud 3 second siren sounds and a red warning flashes. The attempt is logged and recorded, and the PDF says how many times you left and for how long.

If you are not sure about a mark, do not stop to ask your teacher. Mark it wrong if in doubt and ask afterwards.

  • Your attempt number is shown ("ATTEMPT n ON THIS TEST").

  • If a page reloads by accident, reopening the same test within 12 hours picks up where you were. There is a Start afresh option.

  • When you have finished, marked and saved your PDF, you can press 🔄 Take a fresh test at the bottom of the page for new questions with the same settings. The test you have just done is cleared, so save its PDF first. Teacher-set tests do not have this button.

1.8 Writing with the Apple Pencil (Eton PEN)

  • Written answers have two tabs: ✍️ Handwrite (the default) and ⌨️ Type.

  • Pen tools: Pen, Thin / Medium, Eraser, Undo, Clear and + space (adds more room).

  • When the app detects your Pencil, your finger no longer draws: it scrolls the page.

  • Write in black ink. Once marking begins the black ink locks, and a 🖊 Red pen: corrections appears. All corrections go in red. The eraser then removes red only.

  • Typed answers get a red "Corrections" box instead.

1.9 Saving your work to OneNote

Marked papers and timed tests end with 📄 Save my work as PDF (or 📄 Save marked paper as PDF).

  1. The app asks for your name once and remembers it. It also gives you a OneNote page title (date, then a clear title such as "Eton Spot Test on VV10a") with a Copy button.

  2. On an iPad the print screen opens. Do not print. Tap the Share button, then OneNote.

  3. Choose your Div's Class OneNote, then your name, then Class Work (work done in Birley Schools) or EW (work done in your House).

  4. Paste the title you copied, then press Send.

  5. In OneNote, tap the PDF icon and choose Printout so that the pages show in full. (The paper-clip File button only adds a small attachment box.)

On a computer: Save as PDF, then in OneNote choose Insert > File Printout. If the share route fails on an iPad, use Save to Files, then in OneNote Insert > PDF.

1.10 Things to ignore

Every app contains tools that belong to your teacher. You will never need them, and you should leave them alone:

  • The round photo in the header (it switches on teacher mode).

  • Any button marked ✎ Edit, ✏️ Edit Vocab, Print Content, Study Sheet, ⬇ Export HTML or 👩‍🏫 Teacher.

  • Any button starting 🔑 (making test links). You will not even see these unless your teacher has set up a key on that device.

  • The small Teacher button at the foot of the AP Tests page.

Part 2. Tests set by your teacher (AP Tests)

▶ Open AP Tests (opens the app in your browser)

When your teacher sets you a test (in class, or as an EW) you receive either a link or a code. Both open the same thing. Every set test is timed and submitted.

2.1 How to open a set test

  1. With a link (from OneNote or Teams): tap it. The right app opens and your test is ready.

  2. With a code: open AP Tests. You will see Enter your test code. Type the code (for example STF101) and tap Go. The box turns your letters into capitals.

  3. To try the routine without risk, type DEMO101. It is 10 everyday words in French, Spanish and Italian in 1 minute 30. Nobody is expected to know them all.

  • If you see "There is no test called X", check the code with your teacher. Codes your teacher has only just added can take about a minute to work.

  • If you see "The list of test codes could not be loaded", check your internet connection.

  • If a page reloaded part way through, a ▶ Continue your test button appears and your own answers are back.

2.2 What the codes mean

Code starts Kind of test
ST… (e.g. STF101, STS101) A timed Spot Test or past paper. The third letter is the language: F French, S Spanish, I Italian.
SP… (e.g. SPF101, SPS101) A Speed session: the tile game, locked to your teacher's choice.
CLF… and CLI… A Listening class sheet (CLF for French, CLI for Italian; IGCSE only).
RTF… A timed Reading paper (IGCSE only).
ORF…, ORS…, ORI… A recorded oral on one card that your teacher has fixed for you (F French, S Spanish, I Italian; see 2.3).
DRS… (e.g. DRS101) A set drill. The app opens straight into a drill that your teacher has chosen. No timer, nothing logged. Press ✕ Free practice to leave it. So far this is in Eton F Spanish Vocab.

Eton often sets the same set as two or three practice tests (named, for example, "VV11b PRACTICE 1, 2 and 3") for you to do at home, then the ACTUAL TEST in Birley Schools. Your questions may differ from your neighbour's. Between them, the practice tests cover the whole selection your teacher chose, and the ACTUAL TEST is a fresh random draw from all of it, so learning just one practice test will not be enough.

2.3 Doing a set spot test

It works like the timed test in section 1.7, with these differences:

  • There is no untimed option. Press ⏱ Start: N minutes. The clock starts when you tap OK and cannot be paused.

  • The screen says ✅ TEST SET BY YOUR TEACHER with the code.

  • Your PDF carries the code, your times, your attempt number and your level. Use the OneNote page title exactly as the app gives it.

Speed sessions

  • The game opens locked: no list chooser, no way back to the app, no tests and no printing.

  • There is no session timer. You stay until your teacher says stop. ⏹ End session (ask your teacher first) exists, but ask first.

  • Leaving the page sounds the siren and is logged.

Set past papers

A code for an A Level Paper 1 question (for example Q5 Phrases à compléter) sends you to the Eton A Level French Oral Topics & Paper 1 Practice page, timed.

Recorded orals (OR codes)

  • A code starting OR (for example ORI101, an Italian oral) opens the oral app with one card fixed by your teacher. You cannot change it.

  • The 🎙 Record my oral box opens by itself, headed "Set by your teacher (ORI101): this is your card. Record your answers, then follow the steps to send your PDF and recording."

  • Then follow the recording steps your teacher gives you. Put both the PDF and the recording on your EW page in OneNote.

Part 3. F Block Spanish (beginners)

These two apps follow the F Block Spanish course (Zoom Español). Both are Spanish only. Everything that works the same in every app (the drill, the marking keys, Leisure Mode, the Speed Challenge, tests and saving to OneNote) is explained in Part 1. This part tells you how each F Block app looks and what is special about it.

A. The home screen ("Ready to practise?")

  • Direction: English and Spanish, with ⇄ Swap. It starts as English to Spanish. Do that first; then Swap and do Spanish to English.

  • 📚 Choose topics: tick units and sections, or pick a ready-made group: ★ Michaelmas Trials (units 0, 1A, 1B, 2A; this is the default), ★ Lent Half (2B, 3A, 3B), ★ Summer Trials (3B, 4A, 4B, C), 📝 All Regular Chunks, ⭐ All Summer Trials Chunks, or all units. You can also use the Unit and Section drop-downs. A pill shows "Custom selection · N sections".

  • Level: Easy, Medium, Hard (see 1.2). Everything is ticked to start. Easy words look like English; Hard gives you nothing to go on.

  • Cards per session: how many cards you want, or all of them. Timer per card starts at 14 seconds: use − and + to change it.

  • ▶ Start Drill · N cards begins the drill. ☕ Leisure Mode plays the cards for you (see C).

  • ⏱ Autotime (On or Off) only changes the pace in Leisure Mode: with it On, longer cards get longer thinking time.

  • 📝 Generate Test (see E), 📖 View & Print (see F), 📝 View Basic Chunks and ⭐ View Trials Chunks (printable lists of the chunks).

  • The ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge banner is at the top (see D).

B. Drilling

  1. Press ▶ Start Drill. The card shows "English → Español" (or the other way), "Card x of y" and the prompt, which is read aloud. A bar counts down your timer.

  2. Say the answer out loud first. When the bar runs out the answer appears by itself, or press Show Answer. The English is shown under the Spanish.

  3. Mark yourself honestly: ← major error, ↓ nearly (a missing accent or a wrong ending), → got it. Keys here are slightly different from the verb apps: Space or Enter only show the answer. Use the arrow keys to mark.

  4. 🔊 Replay says the card again. The top bar shows your running score (✓ and ✗) and an ▶ Errors button.

  5. At the end, if you had any major errors, the Major Error Drill opens by itself: "N cards to revisit". Keep going until the list is empty.

  • ⚙ Adjust (top bar) pauses the drill and lets you change the direction, then ▶ Apply & resume or ✕ Resume.

  • 🔊 ES speed (side panel): the Spanish voice speed from 50% to 130%, with − and + buttons. Use a slow voice at first, then speed up.

Tip Learn the accents: in a timed test a missing accent costs you a mark (Nearly).

C. ☕ Leisure Mode: learn without a screen

Make your selection, press ☕ Leisure Mode and listen. Each card is read aloud, there is a pause for you to answer, then the answer is shown and read, and the next card follows. It loops for ever. There is no marking and no score. Use Pace − / + to change the thinking pause, ⏸ Pause (or Space) to stop, 🔊 Replay to repeat a card and ⌂ Home to leave. It is excellent on a journey or while you tidy your room: answer out loud before the answer arrives.

D. ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge: "Vocab fast!"

A fast tile game for pairs or small teams. It fills the screen with tiles; each shows a prompt in English. Say the Spanish aloud, then click the tile to check.

  1. On the home screen press the ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge banner. Press 📚 Choose topics ("Which topics are in play?") then ✓ Use these.

  2. Choose the settings: Level (Easy, Medium, Hard, Mixed), Tiles (6, 9, 12 or 16), Time (the length of the game), Teams (Off, 2, 3 or 4) and Each turn (20 seconds, 30, 45 or 1 minute). You can swap the Direction too.

  3. Say the Spanish for a tile out loud. Click the tile to hear and see the correct answer.

  4. Press ✓ Right (the tile greys out and you score) or ✗ Wrong (a new phrase replaces it to try later).

  5. In a team game a wrong answer passes the turn to the next team and the screen shows whose go it is. The team with the most tiles wins.

  • ❓ How to play, 🔀 New set, ⏸ Pause timer, 👁 Reveal all, 🖨 Print sheet and 🔊 Sound on are along the top.

  • At the end you can replay the tiles left on screen, replay the ones that were wrong, or deal a new set.

  • The game has its own 📝 Generate Test, 📋 Eton Spot Test and ⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted buttons. They use the topics and direction you were just playing with.

E. Tests you can make yourself

Press 📝 Generate Test. Choose a Level (Easy, Medium, Hard or Mixed) and the number of Questions. You can then Copy test sheet, Copy answer sheet or 🖨 Print / PDF. Use it for homework or to test a friend.

⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted is a short on-screen test that you mark yourself and save as a PDF to OneNote (see 1.7 and 1.9). Do not leave the page during a timed test or a loud siren sounds. Leave the level on Mixed unless your teacher says otherwise.

F. 📖 View & Print

📖 View & Print shows the words of your chosen topics as a list. Tick 🇬🇧 English and/or 🇪🇸 Spanish to show one column or both (hide one to test yourself), then 🖨 Print / PDF. 📝 View Basic Chunks and ⭐ View Trials Chunks do the same for the useful sentences.

G. If your teacher sets you a drill

Your teacher may send you a link, or a code to type on the AP Tests page (it starts DRS, for example DRS101). It opens this app straight into a drill that your teacher has chosen. A dark bar says "📌 Drill set by your teacher". There is no timer and nothing is logged: do it as normal. Press ✕ Free practice to choose your own topics instead.

Tip Start with the Michaelmas Trials group, English to Spanish, on Easy and Medium. Then Swap and do Spanish to English. Finish with the Major Error Drill.

Five minutes every day is better than an hour once a week.

3.2 Eton F Block Verbs and Grammar

▶ Open Eton F Block Verbs and Grammar (opens the app in your browser)

What it is for: everything for F Block Spanish in one place: conjugating verbs (drills, gap-fill sentences and tests) and beginner grammar (notes, drills and tests). When you open the app you see two big buttons: VERBS and GRAMMAR. Tap one. To come back to the two buttons, tap ⌂ Home. The ← Back button always goes back one step.

Verbs

The drill, Leisure Mode, tests, levels and Speed Challenge work as in Part 1. The differences are below.

A. Choose your verbs

  • F Block Verbs: the verbs used in F Block (aprender, vivir, trabajar, ser, estar, tener, ir, hacer, poder, and reflexives such as levantarse). This is already chosen when you open the app. Start here.

  • All Verbs: about 155 verbs, for when you want more.

  • 🧩 Eton Advanced Structures: about 586 set phrases, conjugated in every tense. For later.

  • ⚡ All Verbs + Structures: both together.

B. Choose a mode

  • 📑 Full (6 forms): the English verb is shown; Show All Forms reveals all six persons. Say them all before you press.

  • 🎯 Single form: one English form such as "we will answer"; you give the Spanish. A Person row (yo, tú, él/ella, nosotros, vosotros, ellos/as) appears in this mode.

  • 🔤 Infinitives: you see the English and give the Spanish infinitive.

  • 💬 Sentences: a gap-fill such as "_______" with a cue like "yo · aprender". Show the answer to see the full sentence.

C. Choose tenses and regularity

  • Tenses: Present (the default), Preterite, Imperfect, Perfect, Future, Conditional, Pres. Subj., Imp. Subj., Infinitive. All Tenses ticks all nine. In F Block start with Present.

  • Regularity: All, Regular, Radical, Irregular. "Radical" means stem-changing or spelling-change verbs.

  • Generate Test: the panel opens under the home screen (use ✕ Close or ⌂ Home to leave). It uses the verb set and tense you chose and picks one random person per verb.

  • The ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge, ☕ Leisure Mode and ⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted work as in Part 1.

Tip Say the whole form aloud before you press Show, for example "yo aprendo". "Nearly" is for a missing accent or a slip in the ending.

Grammar

Tap GRAMMAR on the first screen. Then choose one of three buttons.

  • 📖 NOTES: clear grammar explanations for beginners, with many examples and translations, in the same order as your vocabulary units (Unit 0, 1A, 1B, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, 4A, 4B and the connectors in Section C). Tick the pages you want. Tap a unit name to tick the whole unit, or open the unit to choose single pages. Select all ticks everything and Clear unticks everything. Press ▶ Read to read your pages on screen, or 🖨 Print / save as PDF to print them.

  • ✏️ DRILLS: practice questions with an answer at once. Choose the units, the question types (Mixed, Multiple choice or Fill the gap) and how many questions. In a multiple-choice question tap your answer (or press A, B, C or D). In a gap question type the missing word and press Check. Use the little buttons (á é í ó ú ñ ¿ ¡) to type accents. The app shows why the answer is right. If you are right apart from an accent it says "Nearly" and gives half a mark. At the end you see your score and a list of the ones to go over, each with a button that opens the note to read. Practise the ones I missed repeats only those.

  • 📝 TESTS: choose the units, the question types and the number of questions, then press Make a test. You can print a Student sheet, print an Answer key, or Take it on screen (you see your marks and the corrections at the end). New questions, same settings makes a different test from the same units.

The home screen

  • ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge, Direction menus with ⇄ Swap.

Part 5. Getting the most from the apps

A simple weekly routine

  1. Learn the material for your set (vocab list, VV set, Gr EW chapter or Approved Material topic) with Read & listen, View & Print or Leisure Mode.

  2. Drill it out loud: say the answer before you press Show. Mark honestly.

  3. Re-drill your errors with ▶ Drill major errors again.

  4. Test yourself with ⏱ Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted. Write your What I learned notes properly.

  5. File the PDF in OneNote, in the right folder, with the title copied from the app.

If something goes wrong

Problem What to try
No sound, or the voice sounds poor Tap 🔊 Voices. On an iPad, download the voice in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.
A test code does not work Check the letters and numbers. Wait a minute if your teacher has only just added it. Make sure you are online.
The siren went off You left the test page. It is logged and printed on your PDF. Stay on the page during a test.
The page reloaded mid-test Open the same test again within 12 hours. Press ▶ Continue and your answers return.
I cannot choose a mark Write in red on your answer first.
The microphone bar does not move (oral recording) Wait a few seconds: the app tries other settings. Then press 🎧 Record 4 seconds and play back. If nothing is recorded, check Settings > your browser > Microphone, reload the page and try again.
The PDF button is locked Write your What I learned notes. It unlocks after about 1 minute of writing.
I am not sure how to mark an answer Do not stop the session to ask. Mark it wrong if in doubt. Ask your teacher afterwards.
