Part 1. Read this first: how every Eton app works

All of the Eton apps share the same look, the same buttons and the same keys. Learn them once here and you will know them everywhere. Later parts describe each app on its own, grouped by level and then by language.

1.1 Getting into an app

  • Each app is one web page. Your teacher gives you the link (usually in OneNote or Teams). Open it in your browser on your iPad or computer.

  • Each page name starts with Eton. The exact page name and link are printed at the top of every app's entry in this guide.

  • If your teacher publishes a newer version, the page refreshes itself once. Your saved answers are kept.

  • Tests that your teacher sets you need an internet connection. Pages that only drill are happy once they have loaded.

  • Tap 🔊 Voices (top of every app) to check which voice reads each language. On an iPad the voice sounds far better if you download it first: Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.

  • Tap ⌂ Home to go back to the start screen. It stops any audio.

  • Tap ← Back (next to Home, once you have left the first screen) to go back exactly one step, for example from a drill to its set-up screen, or from a set-up screen to the menu. It also stops any audio.

1.2 The set-up screen (the two steps)

Almost every app opens on a set-up screen with two steps. Nothing is chosen for you: you choose what to practise yourself, and the six practice tiles stay greyed out until you do.

  1. FIRST · What do you want to practise? Choose the set: the unit and section, the lists, the verbs and tenses, or ★ My personalised set (see 1.5a). A line under it says in words what you are about to practise, and how many cards it gives.

  2. NEXT · How do you want to practise it? Press one of the six picture tiles. Each one says what it does underneath.

Tile What it does
Say it Cards one at a time. You say the answer, then mark yourself ✗ ~ ✓ (see 1.3).
Speed Challenge The tile game against the clock, alone or in teams (see 1.5).
Spell it Spelling: hear it and write it accurately (accents count, see 1.5a).
Match it Pair English with the Spanish against the clock (see 1.5a).
Build it Tap or drag the tiles into the right order to rebuild sentences (see 1.5a).
Leisure Mode Hands-free listening (see 1.4).
Test A test to print or do on screen. The answers stay hidden until you press Show answers (see 1.6).

Spell it with Apple Pencil: you can write the answer with Apple Pencil and it is turned into text for checking. Write very clearly and, for difficult words, print the letters separately (no joined-up writing).

Between the two steps is a gold ⚙ Settings bar. It shows your choices in one line; tap Change to open it. The less important controls live here:

Control What it does
Cards per session 10, 20, 30, 50 or All. A "card" is one question.
Timer per card The − and + buttons set how many seconds you get before the answer shows by itself.
⏱ Autotime: On/Off Used in Leisure Mode. On means the pause is counted from the end of the spoken question.
Direction and ⇄ Swap Where the app has them: choose which language you are given and which you must say. ⇄ Swap flips them. Practise both directions.
Mix both ways Puts both directions in one session. 50–50 gives half the cards each way; 70–30 gives seven in ten from English into the other language and three in ten back into English. Each card shows which way it goes. It starts as Off.

Some other controls sit beside the tiles or in FIRST:

Control What it does

📚 Choose several topics (or lists / sections) Opens a tick-box window so that you can mix lists. Click a heading to tick the whole group. Then press ✓ Done at the bottom: you come straight back to the two steps with your topics chosen, ready to pick Drill, Speed Challenge, Spell, Match or Test.

1.3 The self-marking drill

This is the heart of every app. You mark yourself, so be honest. Nobody can see your score and nobody is fooled by an inflated one.

  1. Choose your set in FIRST, then press the Say it tile. A card appears and the question is read aloud. A timer bar shrinks.

  2. Say the answer out loud (or write it). Do this before you look.

  3. Press Show Answer, or press →. The answer appears and is read aloud. Press 🔊 Replay to hear it again. If the timer runs out the answer appears by itself ("Time's up!").

  4. Mark yourself with the three buttons or the arrow keys (table below).

  5. At the end you get your score. Cards you marked as major errors come back for a second go.

Mark Key What happens
✗ Major error ← You did not know it, or it was badly wrong. The card is saved on your error list and comes back in the Major Error Drill.
~ Nearly ↓ Close, with a small slip (an accent, an ending). The card comes back 2 or 3 cards later in the same drill.
✓ Got it → Right. It counts as a point.

Showing the answer: press → (in most apps Space or Enter also work). Then ←, ↓ and → mark the card.

  • The bar at the top shows your running score (✓, ~ and ✗ counts).

  • ▶ Errors opens your error list so far.

  • ⚙ Settings (where you see it) pauses the drill so you can change the direction or the number of cards. Press ▶ Apply & resume to carry on. Settings also lets you change Cards in this drill (the cards you have done stay; the rest are topped up or trimmed) and the Time per card.

  • In a verb Drill the infinitive is hidden, so you practise the conjugation without leaning on it. The 💡 Infinitive button in the top bar switches it On or Off at any moment, even in the middle of a card.

  • In a verb Drill, ⚙ Settings in the top bar pauses the card so you can change the Time per card, switch the infinitive on or off, or change Cards in this drill. Press ▶ Apply & resume to carry on.

  • In the Major Error Drill you can also press ⏭ Ignore to skip a card with no penalty.

The end screen

  • Your score as x / total and a percentage.

  • Either "No major errors: clean sweep" or "N cards to revisit". Copy list copies those cards so you can paste them into OneNote.

  • ▶ Drill major errors again, ↻ Do same set again (all the cards of your selection, not just the first few) and ⌂ Home.

Tip Use Nearly honestly. It is the button that brings a card back quickly, and that repetition is what makes the card stick.

1.4 Leisure Mode (☕)

Leisure Mode is for soaking up the language. Choose your set in FIRST, press the Leisure Mode tile and sit back. Each card is read aloud, there is a pause for you to answer in your head or out loud, then the answer is shown and read, and the next card follows. It loops for ever, reshuffling. There is no marking, no score and no error list.

Tap ⚙ Settings while it plays to pause it and change the direction, the mix or the thinking time; ▶ Apply & resume carries on from the same card.

  • Thinking time: it starts at 4 seconds after the question has been read, so there is no long wait. The − and + buttons change it while it plays: shorter if you are quick, longer for long sentences.

  • ⏸ Pause / ▶ Resume: or press Space.

  • 🔊 Replay repeats the current card. ⌂ Home stops.

Tip Leisure Mode is excellent in the evening, on a walk or on a journey. Try answering out loud before the answer arrives.

1.5 ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge (the tile game)

A fast game for pairs or teams. It fills the screen with numbered tiles. Each tile shows a prompt (usually English).

The bar under the tiles is in three groups: New set, Pause timer, Reveal all and How to play first; the round settings (Level, Tiles, Time, Teams and so on) in the middle, under a This round label; and Print sheet, Sound on and the test buttons last, under More tools.

  1. Choose the settings: Level (Easy / Medium / Hard / Mixed), Tiles (6, 9, 12 or 16), Time, Teams (Off, 2, 3 or 4; click a team name to rename it), Each turn (20 seconds to 1 minute) and Direction. If your list has fewer items than the number of tiles, a short note says so and you get one tile for each item.

  2. Say the number of a tile and then say the answer aloud.

  3. Click the tile (or type its number) to reveal and hear the correct answer.

  4. Press ✓ Right (the tile greys out and you score a point) or ✗ Wrong (a new item replaces it).

  5. Beeps sound at one third and two thirds of the time. A buzzer sounds at the end.

  6. In team games, a wrong answer passes the turn to the next team at once, and a flashing sign shows whose go it is. The team with the most tiles wins.

When the game ends you can ▶ Play the N left on screen, ✗ Play the N that were wrong, 📋 Go through the wrong ones (no timer) or deal a 🔀 New full set.

Other buttons: ❓ How to play, 🔀 New set, ⏸ Pause timer, 👁 Reveal all, 🖨 Print sheet, 🔊 Sound on. The game also has its own No-limit test and Spot Test buttons that use the tiles you were playing with.

A Speed session is the same game but set by your teacher through a link. It is locked to your teacher's choice, starts with 2 teams, 16 tiles, English to the foreign language, 20 seconds a turn and a 3 minute game, and you stay in it until your teacher says stop. There is no leaving it.

1.5a ★ My personalised set, ✍ Spell and 🧩 Match

These are on the set-up screen of the apps, under the two steps.

  • ★ Personalised set. Every card you mark ✗ Major error in a drill, or in Spell, joins your personalised set automatically. ~ Nearly just brings the card back a few cards later: if it still beats you, mark it ✗. Under FIRST · What do you want to practise? tap ★ My personalised set, then choose Say it, Spell it, Match it, Leisure Mode or Test exactly as you would on any other set. The Speed Challenge works on whole lists, so it stays greyed out for this set. Once it is chosen you can narrow it to Hard only, Tricky + hard or Due today.

  • ? How does this work? sits beside ★ My personalised set and gives a short explanation in plain words.

  • ✎ Edit lists your cards. Tap the level (Hard, Tricky, Easier) to change it, or tap ✕ to take a card out.

  • Due today. A card you missed comes back after 1 day, then 3 days, then 7 days. Get it right each time and it stops coming back.

  • 🔗 Save my personalised set gives you a link. Press Copy link and paste it into your own OneNote, under a clear title, and keep that page tidy. Tap that link on any device and the app opens with your set ready. Your set lives only on this iPad, in this browser. If the browser data is cleared, or you use another device, it is gone. The link is the only safe copy, and looking after it is up to you. Paste a fresh link after each big session.

  • ✍ Spell. The app says the Spanish aloud. Type what you hear, or write it in the box with your Apple Pencil (iPadOS turns your handwriting into text; if nothing happens, check that Settings, Apple Pencil, Scribble is on), the sentence checks itself when the last tile goes in; if it is wrong, move the tiles round. Clue: put one tile in for me places one correct tile in a random empty place as an extra clue. Another sentence swaps a sentence you do not like for a new one. Words in green are right, wavy orange is an accent slip, red is wrong or missing. Accents count. Then mark yourself with the usual ✗ Major error ← · ~ Nearly ↓ · ✓ Got it → row. Use 🔊 Listen to hear it again and Show English if you need to check you have understood it — useful for a longer phrase, where it is really a short translation into Spanish as much as a spelling exercise. It uses whichever set you have chosen, and the number of cards you picked in Settings.

  • 🧩 Match. Pair the English cards with the Spanish ones: tap an English card, then its Spanish. A wrong pair adds 3 seconds. The app shows your time and your best time on this iPad. It is a good five-minute warm-up for a Div. Match opens with a two-second picture splash with a quick arcade power-up sound (the 🔊 button turns it off), and the same picture glows faintly behind the game. Each matched pair turns its own colour and makes a soft pop, a wrong pair shakes and adds the 3 seconds, and the timer turns orange and then red as it climbs. Each game opens with a different picture across the top, and a little ghost 👻 races along a bar at your best time, so you can see whether you are ahead of it. Matching several pairs in a row builds a streak, you earn up to three stars at the end, and a new best time gets a celebration. The pairs are not read aloud unless you tap 🗣 Voice off to switch the voice on. The 🔊 button turns the sound effects off.

  • 🧱 Build it. A sentence from your lists is cut into word tiles and some are already in place. Tap or drag the rest into the right order, the sentence checks itself when the last tile goes in; if it is wrong, move the tiles round. Clue: put one tile in for me places one correct tile in a random empty place as an extra clue. Another sentence swaps a sentence you do not like for a new one. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard at the top. If you are stuck, Give me a clue shows the English, and Show me the answer shows the sentence; Hear it reads it aloud. Back returns you to your lists. It uses the full sentences in the lists you chose in FIRST, so the tile stays greyed out if those lists have none.

1.6 Tests you can make yourself

Choose your set, then press the Test tile on the set-up screen. The choices sit in one purple Spot Test card:

Where What it gives you
The big yellow ✍ Do it with Apple Pencil button The Spot Test done on screen with your Apple Pencil, marked by you and handed in as a PDF if required. This is the one to use for serious practice. See 1.7.
The small link Prefer paper? Print it to write on by hand. The same Spot Test as a sheet of 5 boxes with the questions printed on the left and room for your answers on the right. Every question is marked on its own: 2 for perfection, 1 for 75% or more correct, 0 otherwise. You add up the marks yourself: the total is 2 × the number of questions (15 questions is out of 30). The answers are behind a Show answers button.
The discreet link No-limit test (up to the whole set) ▸ at the bottom of the card A printable test of as many questions as you like. You choose 10, 20, 25 or Custom and press Make the test (in the verb apps there is also a Sentences tab; in the Gr EWs apps the link opens the grammar test options directly). The answers stay hidden until you press Show answers. Use Copy test sheet, Copy answer sheet or 🖨 Print / PDF. Good for homework or testing a friend. If your browser blocks the new print window, the sheet appears on the screen instead: tap Print / Save as PDF at the top, or Close to go back.

A Spot Test normally has 15 short questions in 5 boxes (3 per box). The Items per box chips let you choose 5, 10 or 15. You may get fewer if the set is small, or if the answers are long: a timed test never runs past 4½ minutes (the card says so). Grammar (Gr EWs) also starts on 15. When a test panel is open it stands alone on the page with just its Close button, and the header Back button closes it first.

1.7 The timed “Spot Test” with the Apple Pencil, step by step

  1. Press the big yellow ✍ Do it with Apple Pencil button. A box asks you to Choose your level. Leave it on Mixed unless your teacher has told you otherwise. Your level is printed at the top of your PDF.

  2. Choose ⏱ Timed (use this in Birley Schools, or when your teacher has set the test as an EW) or 🏠 Untimed. You cannot change your mind once you start. The clock starts the moment you tap. It can be paused only for a real emergency (a fire alarm, for example): press ⏸ Pause at the top right, type (or write with your Apple Pencil) why you are pausing, then press ▶ Resume. While paused the clock is stopped and the test is hidden, you must still stay on the page (leaving it still sounds the error buzz and is logged), and every pause is printed on your PDF. While a timed test is open your iPad stays awake (it will not dim or lock itself); if you lock it yourself with the side button, that is logged like leaving the page. If the clock at the top of the screen distracts you, tap it to make it smaller (tap again to make it bigger); it stays on screen. Blank answers score 0. No timed test runs longer than 4½ minutes: a test of short answers (words or verb forms) can have up to 20 items, while longer answers give fewer.

  3. Answer each item. Each has two tabs: ✍️ Handwrite (Apple Pencil, black ink) and ⌨️ Type. Only type if you type in exams or have no Pencil.

  4. When finished, press 🔒 I've attempted all N: lock in. In a timed test you get 30 seconds of grace after the time ends, and then your answers lock themselves.

  5. Press ✔ Show answers & mark. Correct your own answer in red first (tick it if perfect, or underline the errors). You cannot choose a mark until there is red on the answer. Once you have used red pen, the app reminds you in red to tap a mark. If those red reminders are too much, press 🔕 Turn off the red “tap your mark” alerts (it appears the first time, or in the marking box); press 🔔 Turn the red alerts back on to bring them back. Your choice is remembered on that iPad. Without the alerts, an unmarked answer still scores 0.

  6. Now give yourself a mark. Single words and forms: 2 Right, 1 Nearly (a missing accent, a spelling slip, a wrong ending or gender), 0 Wrong. Sentences and structures: 4 Perfect, 3 Mostly right, 2 About half right, 1 Only a word or two right, 0 Nothing right. The test is marked out of 20. In a timed test the marking time is limited too, and anything you have not marked scores 0. If you finish marking early, tap I've finished marking: after an Are you sure? check, the marking timer stops and you go straight on to What I learned.

  7. Write in the 📝 What I learned box. This is the most important part: what caught you out, and how will you remember it? In Birley Schools you get a couple of minutes; in your House take as long as you need. Try to find 10 things to write (5 is the minimum), numbered 1 to 10: even a single piece of vocabulary or a rule counts. The PDF button unlocks only after you have written for 1 minute.

  8. Press 📄 Save my work as PDF and file it (see 1.9).

Careful Do not leave the page during a test. If you switch to another app or tab, use Split View, reload or press Back, an error buzz sounds and a red warning flashes. The attempt is logged and recorded, and the PDF says how many times you left and for how long.

If you are not sure about a mark, do not stop to ask your teacher. Mark it wrong if in doubt and ask afterwards.

  • Your attempt number is shown ("ATTEMPT n ON THIS TEST").

  • If a page reloads by accident, reopening the same test within 12 hours picks up where you were. There is a Start afresh option.

  • When you have finished, marked and saved your PDF, you can press 🔄 Take a fresh test at the bottom of the page for new questions with the same settings. The test you have just done is cleared, so save its PDF first. Teacher-set tests do not have this button.

1.8 Writing with the Apple Pencil (Eton PEN)

  • Written answers have two tabs: ✍️ Handwrite (the default) and ⌨️ Type.

  • Pen tools: Pen, Thin / Medium, Eraser, Undo, Clear and + space (adds more room).

  • When the app detects your Pencil, your finger no longer draws: it scrolls the page.

  • Write in black ink. Once marking begins the black ink locks, and a 🖊 Red pen: corrections appears. All corrections go in red. The eraser then removes red only.

  • Typed answers get a red "Corrections" box instead.

1.9 Saving your work to OneNote

Marked papers and timed tests end with 📄 Save my work as PDF (or 📄 Save marked paper as PDF).

  1. The app asks for your name once and remembers it. It also gives you a OneNote page title (date, then a clear title such as "Spot Test on VV10a") with a Copy button.

  2. On an iPad the print screen opens. Do not print. Tap the Share button, then OneNote.

  3. Choose your Div's Class OneNote, then your name, then Class Work (work done in Birley Schools) or EW (work done in your House).

  4. Paste the title you copied, then press Send.

  5. In OneNote, tap the PDF icon and choose Printout so that the pages show in full. (The paper-clip File button only adds a small attachment box.)

On a computer: Save as PDF, then in OneNote choose Insert > File Printout. If the share route fails on an iPad, use Save to Files, then in OneNote Insert > PDF.

1.10 Things to ignore

Every app contains tools that belong to your teacher. You will never need them, and you should leave them alone:

  • The Eton crest in the header (tapping it just says "Not available").

  • Any button marked ✎ Edit, ✏️ Edit Vocab, Print Content, Study Sheet, ⬇ Export HTML or 👩‍🏫 Teacher.

  • Any button starting 🔑 (making test links). You will not even see these unless your teacher has set up a key on that device.

This part tells you how each part of the F Block Spanish app looks and what is special about it.

The app opens on three big buttons: VOCAB, VERBS and GRAMMAR. Tap one. Tap ⌂ Home to come back to the three buttons. The ← Back button always goes back exactly one step, including from the Vocab start screen back to the three buttons.

A. The home screen (the two steps)

The home screen has two steps (see 1.2). Nothing is chosen for you, and the six tiles stay greyed out until you choose what to practise.

  • FIRST · What do you want to practise? Pick a unit and then a section from the two drop-down menus, or press 📚 Choose several topics to tick units and sections, or pick a ready-made group: ★ Michaelmas Trials (units 0, 1A, 1B, 2A), ★ Lent Half (2B, 3A, 3B), ★ Summer Trials (3B, 4A, 4B, C), 📝 All Regular Chunks, ⭐ All Summer Trials Chunks, or all units. A pill shows "Custom selection · N sections". Or choose ★ My personalised set (see 1.5a).

  • Level: Easy, Medium, Hard (see 1.2). Use these chips to narrow the words. Easy words look like English; Hard gives you nothing to go on.

  • NEXT · How do you want to practise it? Press a tile: Say it, Spell it, Match it, ⚡ Speed Challenge, Leisure Mode or Test. They are explained in 1.2 and 1.5a.

  • The gold ⚙ Settings bar (tap Change) holds Cards per session (how many cards you want, or all of them), Timer per card (starts at 14 seconds: use − and + to change it), ⏱ Autotime (On or Off; it only changes the pace in Leisure Mode, with longer cards getting longer thinking time) and Direction: English and Spanish, with ⇄ Swap. It starts as English to Spanish. Do that first; then Swap and do Spanish to English.

  • Mix both ways (also in Settings): Off, 50–50 or 70–30. With 70–30, seven in ten cards go from English into the language and three in ten back into English; each card shows its own direction. It works in the drill, Leisure Mode, tests, Spot Tests and the Speed Challenge, and starts as Off each time.

  • Below the tiles, a small row has 📖 View & Print (see F), 📝 View Basic Chunks and ⭐ View Trials Chunks (printable lists of the chunks).

  • The ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge banner is at the top (see D).

B. Drilling

  1. Choose what to practise, then press the Say it tile. The card shows "English → Español" (or the other way), "Card x of y" and the prompt, which is read aloud. A bar counts down your timer.

  2. Say the answer out loud first. When the bar runs out the answer appears by itself, or press Show Answer. The English is shown under the Spanish.

  3. Mark yourself honestly: ← major error, ↓ nearly (a missing accent or a wrong ending), → got it. Keys here are slightly different from the verb apps: Space or Enter only show the answer. Use the arrow keys to mark.

  4. 🔊 Replay says the card again. The top bar shows your running score (✓ and ✗) and an ▶ Errors button.

  5. At the end, if you had any major errors, the Major Error Drill opens by itself: "N cards to revisit". Keep going until the list is empty.

  • ⚙ Settings (top bar) pauses the drill and lets you change the direction, then ▶ Apply & resume or ✕ Resume.

  • 🔊 ES speed (side panel): the Spanish voice speed from 50% to 130%, with − and + buttons. Use a slow voice at first, then speed up.

Tip Learn the accents: in a timed test a missing accent costs you a mark (Nearly).

C. ☕ Leisure Mode: learn without a screen

Choose what to practise, press the Leisure Mode tile and listen. Each card is read aloud, there is a pause for you to answer, then the answer is shown and read, and the next card follows. It loops for ever. There is no marking and no score. Use Pace − / + to change the thinking pause, ⏸ Pause (or Space) to stop, 🔊 Replay to repeat a card and ⌂ Home to leave. It is excellent on a journey or while you tidy your room: answer out loud before the answer arrives.

D. ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge: "Vocab fast!"

A fast tile game for pairs or small teams. It fills the screen with tiles; each shows a prompt in English. Say the Spanish aloud, then click the tile to check.

  1. On the home screen choose what to practise in FIRST, then press the Speed Challenge tile (or the ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge banner). You can change the topics with 📚 Choose topics ("Which topics are in play?") then ✓ Use these.

  2. Choose the settings: Level (Easy, Medium, Hard, Mixed), Tiles (6, 9, 12 or 16), Time (the length of the game), Teams (Off, 2, 3 or 4) and Each turn (20 seconds, 30, 45 or 1 minute). You can swap the Direction too.

  3. Say the Spanish for a tile out loud. Click the tile to hear and see the correct answer.

  4. Press ✓ Right (the tile greys out and you score) or ✗ Wrong (a new phrase replaces it to try later).

  5. In a team game a wrong answer passes the turn to the next team and the screen shows whose go it is. The team with the most tiles wins.

  • ❓ How to play, 🔀 New set, ⏸ Pause timer, 👁 Reveal all, 🖨 Print sheet and 🔊 Sound on are along the top.

  • At the end you can replay the tiles left on screen, replay the ones that were wrong, or deal a new set.

  • The game has its own 📝 No-limit test, 🖨 Print it and ✍ Spot Test buttons. They use the topics and direction you were just playing with.

E. Tests you can make yourself

Choose what to practise, then press the Test tile. Choose a Level (Easy, Medium, Hard or Mixed) and the number of Questions. You can then Copy test sheet, Copy answer sheet or 🖨 Print / PDF. The answers stay hidden until you press Show answers. Use it for homework or to test a friend.

✍ Do it with Apple Pencil is a short on-screen test that you mark yourself and save as a PDF to OneNote (see 1.7 and 1.9). Do not leave the page during a timed test or an error buzz sounds. Leave the level on Mixed unless your teacher says otherwise.

F. 📖 View & Print

📖 View & Print (a tile after the six practice tiles) shows the words of your chosen topics as a list. Tick 🇬🇧 English and/or 🇪🇸 Spanish to show one column or both (hide one to test yourself), then 🖨 Print / PDF. 📝 View Basic Chunks and ⭐ View Trials Chunks do the same for the useful sentences.

G. If your teacher sets you a drill

Your teacher may send you a link. It opens this app straight into a drill that your teacher has chosen. A dark bar says "📌 Drill set by your teacher". There is no timer and nothing is logged: do it as normal. Press ✕ Free practice to choose your own topics instead.

Tip Start by choosing the Michaelmas Trials group, English to Spanish, on Easy and Medium. Then Swap and do Spanish to English. Finish with the Major Error Drill, then try Spell on your ★ My personalised set.

Five minutes every day is better than an hour once a week.

2.2 Eton F Block Spanish: Verbs and Grammar

▶ Open Eton F Block Spanish (opens the app in your browser on the three big buttons)

What it is for: the verbs and grammar of F Block Spanish: conjugating the 100 Core Verbs (drills, gap-fill sentences and tests) and beginner grammar (Studio, notes, drills and tests). On the three big buttons, tap VERBS or GRAMMAR. To come back to the three buttons, tap ⌂ Home. The ← Back button always goes back one step.

Verbs

The drill, Leisure Mode, the Speed Challenge and tests work as in Part 1. The differences are below. When you tap VERBS the 100 Core Verbs screen opens straight away. It uses the two steps, and nothing is chosen for you.

FIRST, What do you want to practise? You either Choose my own (tick the verb groups: Essential 1 is the first 20 verbs, Essential 2 the second 20, Important the next 30, Useful the last 30; tap See the verbs in these groups to read exactly which verbs they are; then tick the tenses, numbered in the order we learn them: 1 Infinitives, 2 Present, 3 Preterite, 4 Future) or Use a Trials set (press F Michaelmas Trials, which covers Infinitives and Present, or F Block Summer Trials, which covers Infinitives, Present, Preterite and Future: every choice is made for you and locked in, and a note says which verbs and tenses the set covers; Choose my own unlocks it again). Then choose the Kind of question: Trials-style exercises (the four kinds of question on the Trials paper), Single form, Full (6 forms), Infinitives or Sentences. Or choose ★ My personalised set instead. A line under FIRST says in words what you are practising and how many questions it gives.

NEXT, How do you want to practise it? The six tiles stay greyed out until you have chosen verbs and at least one tense. Then press Say it, Spell it (hear the form and write it; accents count), Match it (pair English and Spanish forms, six at a time to begin with (⚙ Settings in the game lets you choose 4 to 12), ⚡ Speed Challenge; it is not used for Sentences, and nor is the Speed Challenge, which plays verb forms and infinitives), Leisure Mode or Test. When the kind of question is Sentences, the Test tile makes a test of gap-fill sentences. The gold ⚙ Settings bar (tap Change) holds Cards per session, Timer per card, Autotime, Hint (shows the infinitive on the card), Regularity and Person. Two more tiles follow the six: 📝 Practice Trials paper (see below) and 📖 View & Print. A test you generate keeps its answers behind Show answers.

Practice Trials paper: on the 100 Core Verbs screen, the 📝 Practice Trials paper tile sits after the six practice tiles. Once you have chosen a Trials set in FIRST, one tap makes the whole paper, exactly like the real one (with Choose my own, the tile makes the F Block Michaelmas paper by default). 🔀 New practice Trials paper makes another. The paper has 40 questions (A To English with Useful verbs, B Change person with Essential verbs, C Infinitive given with Important verbs, D No help with Essential verbs). The F Michaelmas Trials paper uses Infinitives and Present; the F Block Summer Trials paper uses Infinitives, Present, Preterite and Future. It has the same layout as the real Part I, with Name, Div, Date and Score lines on the printed paper. Each paper uses new questions and spreads the six persons across the paper (Change person also varies the new subject). It only uses the tenses and verbs of that Trial, and ignores the verb and tense choices above it. The questions show on their own; press 👁 Show answers when you are ready to mark yourself. Press 🖨 Print / PDF · Paper or 🖨 Print / PDF · Answers to open a printable sheet in a new tab (press ← Back, top left, to close it and return to where you were). To do the paper on the iPad, press ✍ Do it with Apple Pencil under the buttons: choose Timed or Untimed, write your 40 answers in black ink, each question saying what to do, then lock in, open the answers, correct in red and mark yourself out of 40; hand it in as a PDF if required.

Your personalised set on Verbs is kept separately for each kind of question (a missed Single form, a missed Full verb, a missed Infinitive and a missed Sentence). For the personalised set the Speed Challenge and Test tiles are greyed out, and so are the Spot Tests, Practice Papers and View & Print, because they are built from whole verb sets and tenses. Leisure Mode is not used for the Practice Trials paper or the Trials-style exercises. ← Back goes back one stage; press ‹ Eton F Block Spanish to go back to the three big buttons.

A. Choose your verbs

  • 100 Core Verbs: the 100 core verbs, in four groups (Essential 1: 20, Essential 2: 20, Important: 30, Useful: 30), including aprender, vivir, trabajar, ser, estar, tener, ir, hacer, poder, and reflexives such as levantarse. Tap VERBS on the first screen and you are here. Start here.

The numbered questions. Inside FIRST the questions are numbered in the order to answer them: 1 Which verbs, 2 Which tenses, 3 Kind of question and, for Exercises, 4 Which exercises. A yellow line tells you what is still to choose, and the NEXT buttons wake up once everything is answered.

How important is each verb? Under Which verbs in FIRST there are four tick buttons: Essential 1 (20), Essential 2 (20), Important (30) and Useful (30). Tick any mix, or press Essential (40), Essential + Important or All 100. It works on the 100 Core Verbs. Start with Essential 1, the verbs to learn and be able to use yourself, such as ser, estar, tener, haber, hacer and ir, then work through the groups in order. It is about how likely you are to need the verb when you speak or write, not how hard it is (that is Level: Easy, Medium, Hard). The same choice is used in the drill, No-limit test (Conjugation and Sentences tabs), the Speed Challenge and 📖 View & Print, where each verb has an E, I or U badge and Group by: Tier lists them in four groups. In ✍ Do it with Apple Pencil a Which verbs? box comes before the Level box: leave it on all four groups unless your teacher has told you otherwise. What you choose is printed at the top of the test, next to the Level. Level (Easy, Medium, Hard) still works as well, and the two combine. In the Speed Challenge the Importance buttons are in the bar and in 📚 Choose verbs. The choice is forgotten when you close the page.

B. Choose the kind of question

  • 🧩 Trials-style exercises (100 Core Verbs only; the four tick boxes under Which exercises): the four kinds of question on the Trials paper. Tap any mix of them: To English (a Spanish verb form: give the English), Change person (a Spanish verb form and a new subject in brackets: rewrite the verb), Infinitive given (English to Spanish, with the infinitive in [brackets]) and No help (English to Spanish, with no clues). Choose tenses, Regularity and Person as usual, then press Show Answer. The questions are worded exactly as on the practice Trials paper. Leisure Mode is not available for these exercises.

    Which verbs each exercise uses. Under the group buttons a small line, ℹ What each group means in Trials, opens a little table of what each Trials paper expects: on the Trials paper, Essential verbs into Spanish with no help, and change the person; Important verbs into Spanish with the infinitive given; Useful verbs into English only. With Choose my own, every exercise you tick is asked on the groups you ticked. With a Trials set, a switch above the four boxes chooses As in Trials (the default: each box names its verbs, for example "To English · Useful verbs (30)"; each box names the verbs it uses in the real paper) or Any verb, any exercise (harder) (every exercise on all 100 verbs). The switch also decides which verbs go into the tests and Spot Tests you make from that set, and the Practising line says which you are on.

  • 📑 Full (6 forms): the English verb is shown; Show All Forms reveals all six persons. Say them all before you press.

  • 🎯 Single form: one English form such as "we will answer"; you give the Spanish. A Person row (yo, tú, él/ella, nosotros, vosotros, ellos/as) appears in Settings for this kind of question.

  • 🔤 Infinitives: you see the English and give the Spanish infinitive.

  • 💬 Sentences: a gap-fill such as "_______" with a cue like "yo · aprender". Show the answer to see the full sentence.

C. Choose tenses and regularity (Regularity is in Settings)

  • Tenses: Infinitives, Present (the one to start with), Preterite and Future. All ticks all four. In F Block start with Infinitives and Present; your teacher will tell you when to add the Preterite and the Future.

  • Regularity: All, Regular, Radical, Irregular. "Radical" means stem-changing or spelling-change verbs.

  • Test: the panel opens under the home screen (use ✕ Close or ⌂ Home to leave), with the answers behind Show answers. It uses the verb set and tense you chose and picks one random person per verb. With Trials-style exercises as the kind of question, the test (and the Spot Tests) are made of the exercises you ticked, in lettered sections, so you can make a test of, say, Change person and No help only; tick Infinitives in the tenses to include infinitive questions in Infinitive given and No help.

  • The ⚡ Speed Challenge, Spell it, Match it, Leisure Mode and ✍ Do it with Apple Pencil work as in Part 1.

Tip Say the whole form aloud before you press Show, for example "yo aprendo". "Nearly" is for a missing accent or a slip in the ending.

Grammar

Tap GRAMMAR on the first screen. Then choose one of six buttons. In Grammar the app marks your answers for you, so there are no ✗ ~ ✓ buttons and no Show Answer. No units are chosen when you first open DRILLS, TESTS, SPOT TEST or SPEED, so tap the units you want (tap again to untick). All units chooses everything and ✕ Clear all unticks everything; the number next to Questions shows how many you can choose from. If a printed sheet does not open, allow pop-ups.

  • 🎬 STUDIO: the grammar taught step by step, one idea at a time, with a quick check after each and a one-line reason. Tap STUDIO, then choose a chapter. There are 16 chapters, in the same order as your vocabulary units (Unit 0 to the connectors), and the bigger units come in two parts. Each chapter takes about 30 to 40 minutes (the first screen of each chapter tells you exactly how long that one is), and it teaches the vocabulary on those notes pages as well. Many screens have a glowing blue button: press it and watch what happens to the Spanish (a word moves, an ending changes), then press the green button to put it back and read the rule again. Where a word has to go in a sentence, drag the blue word to the gold gap (or tap the gap). At the start of each part you can tap the pieces you want taught, or choose Teach me all of this or I know all of this: test me. Test me skips the teaching and asks 4 questions on that part, the last one the hardest. Get them right and you move on; get the hardest one wrong, or two wrong, and the Studio teaches you the whole part, one screen at a time, and you must use each interactive exhibit before you can carry on. The screen warns you of this before you choose, so choose test me only if you really do know the part. After each part, tick I can now confidently… only when you honestly can, or go back and review. Need more help? opens the grammar notes at the right page, with a button to bring you straight back. Near the end of each part there is a Practise the words for this part screen: press a button such as Click here to practise: Colours and the vocabulary list opens with Drill, Speed Challenge, Spell, Match and Test ready to choose. ← Back to the Grammar Studio (where I was) at the top returns you to the same screen. After it come a few quick questions on those words (what does it mean, how do you say it); any you miss come back at the end of the Focus Point. They are asked even if you choose test me, because the words matter as much as the grammar. The top bar has ← Back, a timer, Pause, My progress sheet and ✕ Exit. My progress sheet asks for your name and shows your time, how far you got and your results, with a big red thoroughness circle from 0 to 100 (it shows how fully you worked, not how many you got right first time). Save as PDF turns it into a PDF to put in OneNote.

  • 📖 NOTES: clear grammar explanations for beginners, with many examples and translations, in the same order as your vocabulary units (Unit 0, 1A, 1B, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, 4A, 4B and the connectors in Section C). Tick the pages you want. Tap a unit name to tick the whole unit, or open the unit to choose single pages. Select all ticks everything and Clear unticks everything. Press ▶ Read (it shows how many pages you ticked, and is greyed out until you tick one) to read your pages on screen, or 🖨 Print / save as PDF to print them. At the end of each page of notes there is a yellow Now I can… box. Tick each line when you can do it, and press Check to see a model answer. Under each part of the notes (and again at the end of the unit) is a ✏️ Practise this part now box with Say it, Test, ✍ Do it with Apple Pencil, 🖨 Print it and Speed, so you can practise each part as soon as you have read it. From the second part onwards you can choose Just this part or This chapter so far before you press a button. Your ticks are remembered on this device. At the end of each unit of notes there is a gold Practise box with four buttons: ✏️ Drill, 📝 Test, ⏱️ Spot Test and ⚡ Speed. Each one starts straight away on that unit, with no set-up screen, and keeps the Difficulty and question type you last chose. (If that choice would leave too few questions, the app uses Mixed.) Press ← Back to return to your notes. The Practise boxes do not appear on a printed copy.

  • ✏️ DRILLS: practice questions with an answer at once. Choose the units, the Difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard, Stretch or Mixed), the question types (Mixed, Multiple choice or Fill the gap) and how many questions. Easy questions test one simple rule. Hard questions combine rules. Stretch is the top level, for future top linguists: whole sentences, spot-the-error and several rules at once. Stretch is not included in Mixed, so choose it on purpose. Try Easy or Medium first and then move up. DIFFICULTY works the same way in TESTS, SPOT TEST and SPEED. In a multiple-choice question tap your answer (or press A, B, C or D). In a gap question type the missing word and press Check. Use the little buttons (á é í ó ú ü ñ ¿ ¡) to type accents. The app shows why the answer is right. If you are right apart from an accent it says "Nearly" and gives half a mark. At the end you see your score and a list of the ones to go over, each with a button that opens the note to read. Practise the ones I missed repeats only those. New drill starts again, and Grammar menu takes you back to the five buttons.

  • 📝 TESTS: choose the units, the question types and the number of questions (5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 40), then press Make a test. You can print a Student sheet, print an Answer key, or Take it on screen (you are not told if you are right until the end, when you see your score and the corrections). New questions, same settings makes a different test from the same units.

  • ⏱️ SPOT TEST: a short grammar test from the units you choose; it starts on 15 questions, and you can choose 5, 10, 15 or 20. Press Make a Spot Test. Then either press Print it (the Student sheet and the Answer key), or press ✍ Do it with Apple Pencil (the bright yellow button) to do it on screen. Choose Timed or Untimed, answer with your Apple Pencil or by typing, lock in, mark yourself and save your work as a PDF, just like the Spot Test in your other apps (you are not asked to choose a level). Each question is either "choose A, B, C or D" or "write the missing word". New questions, same settings gives you a different test.

  • ⚡ SPEED: the Eton Speed Challenge for grammar. It is exactly the same game as the Speed Challenge in the rest of the app (the ring timer, the blurred tiles, How to play, Pause timer, Reveal all, Print sheet and the sound). Press 📚 Choose units to choose the units and the Level (Easy, Medium, Hard, Stretch or Mixed), then use the buttons at the bottom to choose the number of Tiles (6, 9, 12 or 16), the Time and Teams (Off, 2, 3 or 4). If you came from the ⚡ Speed button at the end of a unit of notes, that unit is already chosen. Each tile is a grammar question: a multiple-choice (say A, B, C or D) or a sentence with a gap (say the missing word). Press ▶ Start, say the number of a tile and your answer, then tap the tile to see the answer and press ✓ Right (the tile goes grey and scores) or ✗ Wrong (a new question takes its place). At the end you can play the ones left, play the ones you got wrong, go through them with no timer or deal a new set. 📝 No-limit test, 🖨 Print it and ✍ Do it with Apple Pencil make a test from the same units and level. Press ✕ Back to app to leave the game.

Part 3. Getting the most from the apps

A simple weekly routine

  1. Learn the material for your set (vocab list, VV set, Gr EW chapter or Approved Material topic) with Read & listen, View & Print or Leisure Mode.

  2. Say it it out loud: say the answer before you press Show. Mark honestly.

  3. Re-drill your errors with ▶ Drill major errors again.

  4. Test yourself with ✍ Spot Test. Write your What I learned notes properly.

  5. File the PDF in OneNote, in the right folder, with the title copied from the app.

If something goes wrong

Problem What to try
No sound, or the voice sounds poor Tap 🔊 Voices. On an iPad, download the voice in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.
A link from your teacher does not work Check you have the whole link. Wait a minute if your teacher has only just added it. Make sure you are online. If it still fails, ask your teacher for it again.
The error buzz sounded You left the test page. It is logged and printed on your PDF. Stay on the page during a test.
The page reloaded mid-test Open the same test again within 12 hours. Press ▶ Continue and your answers return.
I cannot choose a mark Write in red on your answer first.
The microphone bar does not move (oral recording) Wait a few seconds: the app tries other settings. Then press 🎧 Record 4 seconds and play back. If nothing is recorded, check Settings > your browser > Microphone, reload the page and try again.
The PDF button is locked Write your What I learned notes. It unlocks after about 1 minute of writing.
I am not sure how to mark an answer Do not stop the session to ask. Mark it wrong if in doubt. Ask your teacher afterwards.
