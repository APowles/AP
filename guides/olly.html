For your degree: master the IPA

▶ Open Eton IPA Studio

https://apowles.github.io/AP/Eton%20IPA%20Studio.html

A practice studio for the International Phonetic Alphabet in French and Italian. It works like the other apps: short, self-marked, and remembered between visits.

  • 🎓 Start here: the Course. If IPA is new or shaky, begin with the Course on the Home screen. It assumes nothing. First come a few short cards on what IPA is, each with a one-question check. Then come lessons of three or four sounds at a time (French has 8, Italian 9). Each lesson goes: meet a sound (an English comparison, how to make it, key words to hear, and a ✗ ~ ✓ for how it felt when you said it aloud), a quick check straight away, a recap where you say honestly whether the sounds feel steady, then mixed practice (including listening), and finally writing the IPA of a few real words.

    You pass a lesson with 80% or more, and are shown your result next to how confident you felt. Anything shaky is gone over again in a short extra round, and you can repeat any lesson. After every second lesson there is a check-in on everything so far, with no pass mark. The Today box at the top suggests the next step and a short review of sounds due another look. Lessons unlock in order, but Try anyway lets you skip ahead. Ten to fifteen minutes a lesson, little and often, is plenty.

  • Start on the Home screen. Choose French or Italian, then tick one or more sound groups (or none for everything). Your choice carries through every activity.

  • 📖 Learn the chart. Every symbol is a tile, coloured by how well you know it. Tap one to see how to make the sound, hear key words with the 🔊 button, and read the warning about the usual trap. Mark yourself ✗ ~ ✓ on the tile page.

  • 🃏 Drill. Flashcards you mark yourself, as in the other apps: press Space to reveal, then ← major error, ↓ nearly, → got it. Choose sound cards, word → IPA, IPA → word, or a mix.

  • ⌨ Transcribe. You see a word and type its IPA using the on-screen keyboard. It is marked at once, and any mistake is shown sound by sound. In Italian the stress mark ˈ counts, and a doubled letter shows a long consonant. Hint tells you how many sounds there are, but then the best you can score is Nearly.

  • 👂 Ear training. You hear one word of a pair that differs by a single sound (tu / tout, pala / palla) and choose which one. Press Slow to hear it more slowly.

  • 📝 Test. Twenty questions marked out of 20, then Save the marked paper as PDF, with your score circled in red.

  • ⚡ Eton Speed Challenge. Race against the clock, solo or in teams, by claiming tiles with correct answers.

  • 🌊 Leisure Mode. Hands-free: words are read aloud and the IPA appears a moment later. No marking, no errors. Good for a walk or while cooking.

  • 📋 Conventions and traps. Read this once for each language. Textbooks differ (for example /ʁ/ or /r/ for the French r), so always follow your own course.

Answers to the 60-second test

  • Passato remoto, io: bevvi, seppi, volli, rimasi, dissi.

  • Italian translations: "Se dipendesse da me, sceglierei di passare le vacanze estive in Sicilia." · "Più ci penso, più trovo che la storia sia molto più interessante della geografia."

  • Subjonctif: que je vaille, que je craigne, que j'acquière, que je cueille.

  • Futur, je: je cueillerai, j'acquerrai, je mourrai.

  • French structures: "si riche que tu sois" (however rich you are) and "à peine étaient-ils partis que le bébé a commencé à pleurer" (no sooner had they left than the baby started crying).

The idea in one minute

Each app is a fast, self-marked drill. A card appears and is read aloud. You say the answer, press Show Answer, and press one of three keys: ← major error, ↓ nearly, → got it. Cards you miss come back until you know them. A drill of 20 cards takes about five minutes.

The point is finding the gaps. Even strong students find holes in the things that were never drilled: vocabulary and genders, the exact irregular forms, the small grammar points. Be strict with the marking and the gaps show up quickly.

Part 3. A routine, and how the drills work

Five 15-minute sessions a week

Day Task Where
Monday Italian verbs: irregular, passato remoto and subjunctives (20 cards, then drill the errors) Eton Italian Verbs
Tuesday French verbs: Specialist Verbs, all tenses Eton French Verbs

Any day Leisure Mode while walking, Speed Challenge with a friend any app

How a drill works

  1. Open the app. FIRST, What do you want to practise?: choose the set yourself (nothing is chosen for you). NEXT, How do you want to practise it?: press Say it. The card is read aloud. The other picture buttons are Speed Challenge, Spell it (hear it and write it, accents count), Match it (pair English with the foreign phrase against the clock), Leisure Mode and Test. The gold Settings bar (press Change) holds cards per session, timer, direction and the other small options.

  2. Once a Drill is running, ⚙ Settings in the top bar pauses the card so you can change the Time per card or the number of Cards in this drill (in the vocabulary Drills also the direction and mix). Press ▶ Apply & resume to carry on. In Leisure Mode, the same ⚙ Settings button pauses it and lets you change the thinking time.

  3. ★ My personalised set collects anything you mark ✗ Major error in a drill, or get wrong in Spell. Filter it with All, Hard only, Tricky + hard or Due today; 🔗 Save my personalised set gives a link to paste into OneNote. Speed Challenge is not offered for it.

  4. Say the answer out loud first. Then press Show Answer (or the → key).

  5. Mark yourself: ✗ Major error (←), ~ Nearly (↓) or ✓ Got it (→). Be strict. A missing accent is "Nearly".

  6. At the end press ▶ Drill major errors again. Use Copy list to paste your errors into Notes.

Things worth knowing

  • Sound: the voice reading each card is your device's text-to-speech. It sounds much better with an enhanced voice installed (on a Mac or iPhone: Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content). Tap 🔊 Voices in any app to check.

  • Scores are not saved. Use Copy list to keep the cards you got wrong.

  • Ignore: the Eton crest in the header (tapping it just says "Not available"), any button marked ✎ Edit, Print Content or 🔑, and the "Save my work as PDF" and OneNote steps at the end of timed tests. Those belong to your teacher's Divs.

  • Tests: a Test never shows its answers until you press Show answers, so mark yourself honestly. 📝 No-limit test makes a printable test. ✍ Do it with Apple Pencil is a short on-screen test that you mark yourself (choose Untimed to take it at your own pace). In a timed test, do not leave the page: an error buzz sounds. If something urgent happens (a fire alarm, for example), press ⏸ Pause at the top right, type why, then press ▶ Resume; the pause is printed on your PDF. While a timed test is open your iPad stays awake (it will not dim or lock itself); if you lock it yourself with the side button, that is logged like leaving the page. If the clock at the top of the screen distracts you, tap it to make it smaller (tap again to make it bigger); it stays on screen. When you mark yourself, use red pen on your answer and then tap a mark; if the red reminders to tap your mark are too much, press 🔕 Turn off the red “tap your mark” alerts (press 🔔 Turn the red alerts back on to bring them back). An unmarked answer still scores 0. When you have saved your PDF you can press 🔄 Take a fresh test at the bottom of the page for new questions with the same settings (the test you have just done is cleared, so save its PDF first). No timed test runs longer than 4½ minutes: a test of short answers (words or verb forms) can have up to 20 items, while longer answers give fewer. Where your teacher's list gives more than one accepted answer for an English prompt (for example "living room": la salle de séjour / le salon), the mark scheme now shows them all. Spellings that are right either way are listed too (for example l'aller simple, with or without the hyphen).

  • Levels: most apps have Easy / Medium / Hard. Easy looks like English, Medium looks like a different English word, Hard gives you nothing to go on. Start on Hard to find the gaps, then clear the rest.
