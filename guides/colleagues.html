The Eton Apps: Quick Start for Colleagues

Modern Languages and Cultures · October 2026

These are optional. They are there if you find them useful for your own classes. There is nothing you have to do or use.

What they are

A set of web pages the boys already use for vocabulary, verbs, grammar, listening and oral practice. They work in Safari on an iPad (and on the projector). There is nothing to install and no log-in. Open the page, choose what to practise, and go.

Three ways to use them

  1. In a lesson. Put an app on the projector and use a drill or a quick test as a starter or plenary.
  2. As a link for the boys. Choose the vocabulary list or grammar recap you want in an app, tap the copy button and paste the link into your class notebook (OneNote) or Teams. A boy who taps it lands straight on that content with the drill, test and speed buttons ready.
  3. As a short test. In an app, start a Spot Test (Eton Spot Test: timed & submitted), then tap Make a link to share this test. Paste the link where your boys will find it. They do it on the iPad with the Apple Pencil, mark it themselves and hand it in as a PDF. Each link gives every boy his own draw of the questions.

The material has not changed

The content is exactly what you already know. Only the way the boys practise it is new.

  • Grammar EWs (non-specialists and A Level). The recaps and exercises follow the same grammar prescription as the pamphlets. The exercises themselves are new, written to that same prescription, so they replace the old pre-test exercises without changing what is being taught.
  • IGCSE vocabulary EWs. These are the same vocabulary prescriptions as before, with the same set names.

Differentiation: the real gain

  • Vocabulary is graded. Every word is graded by difficulty, and you or the boys can sort and choose by it: Easy looks like English (famille), Medium looks like a different English word (appartement), Hard gives nothing to go on (manger). A stronger boy can go straight to the Hard words, and a weaker one can build confidence on the Easy ones.
  • Grammar is graded in the same way. The different kinds of sentence and exercise that can be used to test each grammar point have all been graded, so you can set an easier or harder test on the same point for different boys in the same class.

A Level specialist vocabulary (the old VVs)

The old VVs are now organised by topic, Topics 1 to 12, in weekly sets (for example TV1a, TV1b, TV1c). Each week has two parts:

  • Topic vocabulary, on its own or with the week’s verb structures.
  • Verb structures, split into Essential (the five most important of the week, to be written in French), Important (the next five) and Recognition (all the others, to be understood).

The usual weekly choice is the topic vocabulary plus the five Essential verb structures. The app opens on it.
