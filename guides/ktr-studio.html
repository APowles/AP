How to use the KTR Studio

The KTR Studio is where you learn the 70 Key Topic Records (KTRs) for the Oral (Paper 3) and Paper 1. Each KTR is one small topic, such as Le mariage pour tous. This guide shows how to get the most from it. You do not need to do everything: follow the route below.

1. The route for one KTR

Every KTR opens with its picture filling the screen, which softens to a watercolour as the hook question appears for a moment (it stays until you tap anywhere to continue), and then settles into the Overview, which opens on Read & listen so you hear the KTR before you rehearse it. At the top of each KTR the numbered route lists the stages in order: tap any of them and it opens that stage and scrolls you straight down to where it begins. Under the tabs, a gold line says what the stage you are on is for, with a button on its right that takes you to the next stage. The stages come one at a time, so you meet the KTR before you practise it. The row of tabs (Overview, Go deeper, Key facts, Vocab, Drill it, Use it, Now I can, Practise it) shows where you are, and the Next button at the foot of each stage takes you on to the following one. The last stage, Practise it, is where the choices for how to practise this KTR are. Use it opens with three short steps (read the question and tick what you would use, say your answer aloud from the frame, then show a model sentence and compare). Under the Overview, Glossary: the key terms of this KTR explains the technical words. On Key facts, a ▶ Read all aloud button reads every fact in French, one after another, highlighting each as it goes (press it again to stop), and a row of Jump to buttons at the top takes you straight to people, dates, figures and the other sections. Some facts also have a Click here for explanatory gloss button under them. Tap it to open a short English explanation of the background to that fact; tap it again to close it. The gloss stays closed until you ask for it, so the page is not crowded.

🎯 Dans le mille: under every KTR’s question there is a gold-edged button with a target on it, closed until you tap it. It opens a note that says, in a sentence or two, what a good answer to that question must explain. Have a go at the question first, then open it to check that you covered what matters. You will also find it beside La réponse when the wheel’s time is up, in 📖 Help me prepare this, just before La réponse at the end of the story, at the top of Use it, on the whole-KTR card in Shuffle and deal, and on its own screen in Project.

  1. The six boxes of Read & listen. Each part has its own soft colour, and a curly arrow runs from 1 to 2 to 3 to 4 to 5 to 6 so the order is clear. Press 🖥 Project to show them big for the projector: 1 per screen or 2 per screen, with ‹ Previous and Next › (or the left and right arrow keys), the 🔊 button on each box, and the See setting (Français, English, Both) respected. Press Project again, Esc or Back to return to the grid.
  2. The picture on Go deeper. 🔢 Note the following details brings up the numbers. 🎧 Listen to the model plays the 30-second model in French at once (press it again to stop), 🇬🇧 Listen to the model in English does the same in English, and 📝 Show the words shows the text over the picture (press 🖼 Back to the picture to see the picture again).
  3. Weekly topic vocabulary. The topic vocabulary you learn each week (the VV sets TV1a to TV12c) now appears in the Vocab tab of the matching KTR, labelled with its set (for example VV7A). Only vocabulary specific to a KTR is included; all-purpose phrases are not. It also feeds Spell, Match and Speed.
  4. Learn it. Work through the tabs in order: Overview, Go deeper, Vocab, Drill it, Use it. Do this in School with your Div, as a revision activity, or perhaps as an EW. On the Pétain, Vichy, Statut des Juifs, de Gaulle, Moulin, Resistance radio and Chirac KTRs, some Overview parts also have 🎵 buttons that play a short clip of the real speaker (for example Pétain on 17 June 1940, Malraux at the Panthéon, Chirac in 1995); press a button again to stop it at once. The de Gaulle clip is a modern reading, because the original of 18 June was never recorded.
  5. Remember it. Open Key facts. Read it, then tick every card you can say from memory. Anything you cannot say stays unticked: that is your revision list.
  6. Say it out loud. Under Practise this KTR, choose Les idées clés, then do Drill it and Spell it.
  7. Check it in a few days. Come back later in the week for the Spot Test, Speed session and Match it.

Then return in two weeks and do Drill it again. A short return visit does far more than a long session once.

2. The À retenir page

It is a picture guide to the facts you need for the Oral:

  • Qui ? the people, with a photo and one line on each.
  • Quoi ? what things are (an organisation, a law, a place).
  • Quand ? the dates.
  • Combien ? the figures.
  • Les phrases clés key lines from speeches (on some KTRs). Some have a 🎵 Listen button that plays a short clip of the speaker; press it again (it now says ■ Stop) to stop at once. Under the line, a short note says who is speaking, when, what it is about and why it matters. Nothing plays by itself. Tap the note to flip it between French and English.
  • Les idées clés the key ideas to use in your Oral answer.

Tap the speaker to hear any line, or press Play all above Les idées clés to hear all the key ideas one after another (press it again to stop): good for a walk or the bus. Each key idea is a claim you can use in the oral, with the facts that back it up in brackets underneath, in French. Tap an idea to see the English (of the idea and of its facts); tap again to go back to French. Tick a card when you can say it without looking. Pictures marked reconstitution are made to illustrate the idea, not photographs from the time.

3. The practice tiles

At the bottom of the page, under Practise this KTR, there are two steps. FIRST, the switch, decides what you practise: the pale green Whole KTR button (everything), or tick any of Recap the story, Picture facts (the facts behind the numbers on the picture), Les idées clés, Qui · Quand · Quoi, All “Now I can” points and Quick revision. You can tick several and they combine. NEXT, the tiles, decide how you practise it. A tile that does not fit your choice is greyed out, with the reason underneath.

TileWhat it is for
Drill itOral drill, the same as everywhere else in the apps: you see the English (with a timer), say the French aloud, press Show Answer, and mark yourself. The English question is read to you and the French answer plays when it shows; the Autoplay button in the top bar switches both off and on. The best way to rehearse your Oral.
Spell itAccuracy: you hear it and write it, every accent right. You can write with Apple Pencil. A perfect answer gets a big green tick and moves on by itself; the 🎚 Speed & pauses button at the top slows the voice down.
Match itFind the pairs against the clock. With Qui · Quand · Quoi you match each description to the person, date or thing.
Build itPut the tiles in order to build the sentence.
Speed sessionRace the clock: how many can you get right? Good for checking, not for learning something new.
Roll itOral Cycle practice. The dice roll and give you a phrase from the Oral Cycle and a fact from this KTR (the facts follow the switch above: the whole KTR, Now I can points, Les idées clés, or Qui · Quand · Quoi). Say a French sentence aloud that uses the phrase to talk about the fact, then check and mark yourself. Do it in School with your Div, or on your own. Letters can be switched off, and Hard hides the English meaning of the phrase.
Spot TestShort questions answered with Apple Pencil. When you choose Les idées clés or Now I can, the test is made straight away, after you pick a level.

Answers are never shown first: you always check them yourself, then mark yourself honestly.

4. Revising a whole sub-theme

Each sub-theme page (for example Les structures familiales) has a Practise across KTRs panel. Tick the KTRs you want to combine (or All), then use the same tiles on all of them at once. This is the best way to revise before a test.

Below it, Frise chronologique · Timeline lists every date from all the KTRs of that sub-theme in time order. Tap the speaker to hear a date, or tap the KTR name to go to it.

View & Print

When you choose View & Print in the practice choices, the sheet opens on screen first. Along the top, Show lets you see Both languages, French only or English only. Cover hides the French (or the English) behind a grey bar: say it aloud, then tap the bar to reveal it, and tap again to cover it. Reveal all shows everything at once. Print / PDF prints the sheet as you have set it, so a covered sheet prints with blank answer lines. Each line has its own 🔊 FR and 🔊 EN buttons, so you can hear it whether it is covered or not. Once a line is revealed, tapping the text also reads it aloud.

5. One sheet to print

At the bottom of Key facts, one A4 summary sheet shows the whole page on a single sheet: people, things, dates, figures and key ideas. Press Print / Save as PDF. Keep it in your folder and read it the night before a lesson.

6. A weekly pattern that works

  • In School: Learn it (steps 1 and 2).
  • Same evening, 10 minutes: À retenir, then Drill it with Les idées clés.
  • Two or three days later, 10 minutes: Spell it, then Match it.
  • End of the week: Spot Test and Speed session.
  • Two weeks later, 5 minutes: Drill it again.

If something is not working

Voice too fast? Tap 🎚 Speed & pauses in the navy bar at the top and move the Speed slider. Print screen shows other things? Tap Back, then press the print button again. A tile is greyed out? Read the short reason under it: usually you only need to change the switch above the tiles.
