How to use the KTR Studio

The KTR Studio is where you learn the 70 Key Topic Records (KTRs) for the Oral (Paper 3) and Paper 1. Each KTR is one small topic, such as Le mariage pour tous. This guide shows how to get the most from it. You do not need to do everything: follow the route below.

1. The route for one KTR

At the top of every KTR you will find a card called Suggested route for this KTR. Tick each step when you have done it. The card remembers your ticks on this device.

  1. Learn it. Work through the tabs in order: Overview, Go deeper, Words, Drill it, Use it. Do this in School with your Div, as a revision activity, or perhaps as an EW.
  2. Remember it. Open À retenir. Read it, then tick every card you can say from memory. Anything you cannot say stays unticked: that is your revision list.
  3. Say it out loud. Under Practise this KTR, choose Les idées clés, then do Drill it and Spell it.
  4. Check it in a few days. Come back later in the week for the Spot Test, Speed session and Match it.

Then return in two weeks and do Drill it again. A short return visit does far more than a long session once.

2. The À retenir page

It is a picture guide to the facts you need for the Oral:

  • Qui ? the people, with a photo and one line on each.
  • Quoi ? what things are (an organisation, a law, a place).
  • Quand ? the dates.
  • Combien ? the figures.
  • Les idées clés the key ideas to use in your Oral answer.

Tap the speaker to hear any line, or press Play all above Les idées clés to hear all the key ideas one after another (press it again to stop): good for a walk or the bus. Tick a card when you can say it without looking. Pictures marked reconstitution are made to illustrate the idea, not photographs from the time.

3. The practice tiles

At the bottom of the page, under Practise this KTR, there is a switch and seven tiles. The switch decides what you practise: Whole KTR, All Je peux points, Les idées clés, or Qui · Quand · Quoi. A tile that does not fit your choice is greyed out, with the reason underneath.

TileWhat it is for
Drill itOral drill: you see the English and say the French aloud, check, and mark yourself. The best way to rehearse your Oral.
Spell itAccuracy: you hear it and write it, every accent right. You can write with Apple Pencil.
Match itFind the pairs against the clock. With Qui · Quand · Quoi you match each description to the person, date or thing.
Build itPut the tiles in order to build the sentence.
Speed sessionRace the clock: how many can you get right? Good for checking, not for learning something new.
Roll itOral Cycle practice. The dice roll and give you a phrase from the Oral Cycle and a fact from this KTR (the facts follow the switch above: the whole KTR, Je peux points, Les idées clés, or Qui · Quand · Quoi). Say a French sentence aloud that uses the phrase to talk about the fact, then check and mark yourself. Do it in School with your Div, or on your own. Letters can be switched off, and Hard hides the English meaning of the phrase.
Spot TestShort questions answered with Apple Pencil. When you choose Les idées clés or Je peux, the test is made straight away, after you pick a level.

Answers are never shown first: you always check them yourself, then mark yourself honestly.

4. Revising a whole sub-theme

Each sub-theme page (for example Les structures familiales) has a Practise across KTRs panel. Tick the KTRs you want to combine (or All), then use the same tiles on all of them at once. This is the best way to revise before a test.

Below it, Frise chronologique · Timeline lists every date from all the KTRs of that sub-theme in time order. Tap the speaker to hear a date, or tap the KTR name to go to it.

5. One sheet to print

At the bottom of À retenir, one A4 summary sheet shows the whole page on a single sheet: people, things, dates, figures and key ideas. Press Print / Save as PDF. Keep it in your folder and read it the night before a lesson.

6. A weekly pattern that works

  • In School: Learn it (steps 1 and 2).
  • Same evening, 10 minutes: À retenir, then Drill it with Les idées clés.
  • Two or three days later, 10 minutes: Spell it, then Match it.
  • End of the week: Spot Test and Speed session.
  • Two weeks later, 5 minutes: Drill it again.

If something is not working

Voice too fast? Tap 🎚 Speed & pauses in the navy bar at the top and move the Speed slider. Print screen shows other things? Tap Back, then press the print button again. A tile is greyed out? Read the short reason under it: usually you only need to change the switch above the tiles.
